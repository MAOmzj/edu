小学英语分级词汇与句型训练（1-6年级）

本资料按年级整理英语核心词汇和常用句型，每级配单词表、重点句型和情景对话。词汇是英语的砖石，句型是英语的框架——积累词汇，掌握句型，就能开口说英语。

============================================================
一年级
============================================================

【核心词汇（约100词）】

数字：one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten
颜色：red, yellow, blue, green, white, black, pink, orange, purple, brown
动物：cat, dog, bird, fish, duck, pig, rabbit, monkey, panda, elephant
身体：head, eye, ear, nose, mouth, hand, arm, leg, foot, body
水果：apple, banana, orange, pear, peach, watermelon, grape, strawberry
家人：father/dad, mother/mum, brother, sister, grandpa, grandma

【重点句型】
Hello! / Hi! 你好！
What's your name? My name is... 你叫什么名字？我叫……
How are you? I'm fine, thank you. 你好吗？我很好，谢谢。
How old are you? I'm... 你几岁了？我……岁。
What color is it? It's... 它是什么颜色？它是……色的。
I like... 我喜欢……
I have a... 我有一个……

【情景对话】
见面问好：
A: Hello! I'm Amy.
B: Hi! I'm Tom. Nice to meet you!
A: Nice to meet you, too!

介绍颜色：
A: What color is your bag?
B: It's blue. I like blue.
A: Me too!

============================================================
二年级
============================================================

【新增词汇（约150词）】

学校：book, pen, pencil, ruler, eraser, bag, desk, chair, teacher, student
食物：bread, milk, egg, rice, cake, juice, water, noodles, chicken, fish
衣服：shirt, T-shirt, dress, skirt, pants, shorts, shoes, socks, hat, coat
天气：sunny, cloudy, rainy, snowy, windy, hot, cold, warm, cool
玩具：ball, doll, kite, car, boat, robot, teddy bear, puzzle

【重点句型】
What's this/that? It's a... 这是什么/那是什么？它是一个……
Where is the...? It's in/on/under... ……在哪里？它在……里面/上面/下面。
Do you like...? Yes, I do. / No, I don't. 你喜欢……吗？是的/不是。
Can I have some...? Sure. Here you are. 我能要些……吗？当然，给你。
How many...? ...个/只……？
Let's... 让我们……

【情景对话】
在教室：
A: Where is my pencil?
B: It's on the desk.
A: Oh, thank you!

买食物：
A: Can I have some milk, please?
B: Sure. Here you are.
A: Thank you!

三年级
============================================================

【新增词汇（约200词）】

学科：Chinese, English, math, music, art, PE, science
职业：teacher, doctor, nurse, farmer, driver, police officer, singer, dancer
时间：Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
月份：January-December（12个月份）
季节：spring, summer, autumn/fall, winter
位置：in, on, under, behind, in front of, next to, between, near

【重点句型】
What day is it today? It's... 今天星期几？今天是……
What time is it? It's... o'clock. 现在几点？……点钟。
What's the weather like? It's... 天气怎么样？……
How do you go to school? I go by... 你怎么上学？我乘……去。
What would you like? I'd like... 你想要什么？我想要……
Where are you from? I'm from... 你从哪里来？我来自……

【情景对话】
谈论天气：
A: What's the weather like today?
B: It's sunny and warm.
A: Let's go to the park!
B: Good idea!

问路：
A: Excuse me, where is the library?
B: Go straight and turn left. It's on your right.
A: Thank you!

四年级
============================================================

【新增词汇（约250词）】

家庭活动：cook, clean, wash, sweep, make the bed, do homework
日常活动：get up, go to school, have lunch, do sports, watch TV, go to bed
形容词：tall, short, thin, fat, strong, kind, strict, funny, clever, shy
动词过去式：went, came, saw, ate, drank, played, watched, read, wrote
自然：mountain, river, lake, forest, field, island, beach

【重点句型】
What's he/she like? He's/She's... 他/她是什么样的人？他/她……
What do you have on...? I have... 你……有什么课？我有……
What would you like for...? I'd like... ……你想吃什么？我想要……
What can you do? I can... 你会做什么？我会……
What are you doing? I'm...ing. 你在做什么？我正在……
What did you do yesterday? I...ed. 你昨天做了什么？我……了

【情景对话】
谈论周末：
A: What did you do last weekend?
B: I visited my grandparents. We had a big dinner.
A: That sounds great!

描述人物：
A: What's your English teacher like?
B: She's tall and thin. She's very kind.
A: Do you like her?
B: Yes, very much!

五年级
============================================================

【新增词汇（约300词）】

家务：sweep the floor, cook the meals, wash the clothes, clean the room
日常：brush teeth, wash face, comb hair, get dressed
自然现象：rain, snow, wind, cloud, fog, thunder, lightning
情感：happy, sad, angry, tired, excited, bored, scared, surprised
交通：bus, car, bike, plane, train, ship, subway, taxi

【重点句型】
What do you do on...? I often... 你在……做什么？我经常……
Which season do you like best? I like... best. 你最喜欢哪个季节？我最喜欢……
What's your favorite...? My favorite... is... 你最喜欢的……是什么？我最喜欢的……是……
When is...? It's in... ……在什么时候？在……
Why do you like...? Because... 你为什么喜欢……？因为……
How do you feel? I feel... 你感觉怎么样？我感觉……

【情景对话】
谈论爱好：
A: What's your hobby?
B: I like reading books. What about you?
A: I like playing basketball. Let's play together!
B: Great!

谈论季节：
A: Which season do you like best?
B: I like spring best. The weather is warm and the flowers are beautiful.
A: Me too! Spring is my favorite season, too.

六年级
============================================================

【新增词汇（约300词）】

学校生活：exam, test, grade, subject, homework, project, presentation
职业（拓展）：engineer, scientist, artist, writer, reporter, actor, actress
情感（拓展）：worried, nervous, proud, grateful, ashamed, confused
环境：pollution, recycle, protect, environment, energy, waste
未来：future, dream, plan, hope, wish, goal

【重点句型】
What are you going to be? I'm going to be a... 你将来想当什么？我想当……
How do you go to...? I go by... 你怎么去……？我乘……去
What did you do last...? I... 上个……你做了什么？我……了
Where did you go? I went to... 你去了哪里？我去了……
How was your...? It was... 你的……怎么样？很……
What should I do? You should... 我应该做什么？你应该……

【情景对话】
谈论未来：
A: What are you going to be in the future?
B: I'm going to be a doctor. I want to help sick people.
A: That's a great dream! How are you going to do that?
B: I'm going to study hard and go to medical school.

谈论旅行：
A: Where did you go last summer?
B: I went to Beijing with my family. We visited the Great Wall.
A: How was it?
B: It was wonderful! The Great Wall is really amazing.

词汇记忆方法
============================================================

分类记忆：把同一类的词放在一起记（如水果、动物、颜色）。
联想记忆：把单词和图像联系起来（如apple想象一个红苹果）。
语境记忆：在句子中记单词，不要孤立地背。
制作单词卡：正面写英文，背面写中文和例句，随时翻看。
每天复习：今天学10个新词，同时复习昨天的10个。

坚持每天积累，词汇量就会像滚雪球一样越来越大。有了词汇的基础，再加上句型的框架，你就能自信地开口说英语了！
