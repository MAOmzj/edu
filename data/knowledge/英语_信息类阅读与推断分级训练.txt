# 小学英语信息类阅读与推断分级训练

资料性质：原创学习补充材料，不对应某一出版社或教材版本。
适用年级：小学三至六年级。
检索关键词：English reading, notice, timetable, menu, poster, main idea, detail, inference, reference word, 信息阅读。

## Reading strategy

Read the title first. Then read the question and look for key words in the text. For a detail question, copy the meaning accurately. For an inference question, use clues from the text and do not add facts that are not given.

## Text 1: Art Club Notice — Grade 3-4

ART CLUB OPEN DAY

Date: Friday, 20 September
Time: 3:30 p.m.—5:00 p.m.
Place: Room 204
Activities: leaf printing, paper animals and a mini art show
Bring: an old T-shirt and a water bottle
Important: Students under nine must come with an adult.

### Questions

1. Where is the open day?
2. How long does it last?
3. Why should students bring an old T-shirt?
4. Can an eight-year-old student come alone?

### Answers

1. It is in Room 204.
2. It lasts one and a half hours.
3. The old T-shirt can protect their clothes during art activities. This is a reasonable inference from the activity and the item to bring.
4. No. Students under nine must come with an adult.

## Text 2: School Lunch Menu — Grade 3-4

| Day | Main food | Vegetables | Fruit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | rice and chicken | carrots | apple |
| Tuesday | noodles and egg | cabbage | banana |
| Wednesday | rice and fish | green beans | orange |

Ben does not eat fish. Lucy wants a lunch with an orange. The dining hall opens from 11:30 a.m. to 12:40 p.m.

### Questions

1. What fruit is served on Tuesday?
2. Which day's main food should Ben avoid?
3. On which day can Lucy get an orange?
4. If Sam arrives at 12:45 p.m., can he have lunch in the dining hall?

### Answers

1. A banana.
2. Wednesday's main food.
3. Wednesday.
4. No. The dining hall closes at 12:40 p.m.

## Text 3: The Bottle Garden — Grade 4-5

Our class made a small garden in a clear bottle. First, we put small stones at the bottom. Then we added soil and planted two tiny ferns. We gave them a little water and closed the bottle.

For the first week, drops of water appeared inside the glass every morning. Later, the soil became dry because the lid was not closed tightly. We closed it again and moved the bottle away from strong sunlight. The ferns became green again after several days.

### Questions

1. What did the class put into the bottle before the soil?
2. Why did the soil become dry?
3. What does “it” in “We closed it again” refer to?
4. What two actions helped the ferns become green again?
5. What is the main idea?

### Answers

1. Small stones.
2. Because the lid was not closed tightly.
3. The lid.
4. The students closed the lid tightly and moved the bottle away from strong sunlight.
5. The text explains how the class made and cared for a bottle garden.

## Text 4: A Different Route — Grade 5-6

Maya usually cycles to the sports centre along River Road. On Saturday morning, a sign said, “Road closed from 9:00 to 12:00 for tree work.” Her basketball lesson began at 10:00.

Maya looked at the town map. The park path was shorter, but bicycles were not allowed there. Hill Street was open, but it had a steep climb. She chose Hill Street and left home fifteen minutes earlier than usual. She arrived safely at 9:50.

### Questions

1. Why couldn't Maya use her usual route?
2. Why didn't she choose the shorter park path?
3. What difficulty did Hill Street have?
4. What can we infer about Maya?
5. Which detail best supports the answer to Question 4?

### Answers

1. River Road was closed for tree work.
2. Bicycles were not allowed on the park path.
3. It had a steep climb.
4. She planned carefully and cared about safety and time.
5. She checked the map, chose an allowed route and left fifteen minutes early.

## Text 5: Saving Water at School — Grade 5-6

The eco team checked how much water three classroom taps wasted in one hour. Tap A dripped 120 millilitres, Tap B dripped 450 millilitres, and Tap C did not drip. The team reported the problem instead of trying to repair the taps themselves. A worker replaced a small part in Tap B and tightened Tap A.

One week later, the team checked again. Neither tap dripped. The students then made a sign: “Turn the tap off fully. Tell an adult if it keeps dripping.”

### Questions

1. Which tap wasted the most water?
2. How much more water did Tap B waste than Tap A in one hour?
3. Why did the students report the problem instead of repairing the taps?
4. What evidence shows that the repair worked?
5. Give the text a suitable title.

### Answers

1. Tap B.
2. 450－120＝330 millilitres.
3. Tap repair should be done safely by a responsible adult or worker.
4. One week later, neither tap dripped.
5. Example: The Eco Team Stops Dripping Taps.

## Useful answer frames

- The text says that …
- I think … because …
- The word “it/they/this” refers to …
- The main idea is that …
- First …, but …, so …

Check your answer: Did I answer the exact question? Did I use evidence? Did I avoid adding information that the text never gives?
