# 小学英语语法与句型分级训练

## 第一篇：低年级语法训练（1-2年级）

### 一、be动词（am/is/are）

#### 1. 基本用法
- I am... 我是……
- You are... 你是……
- He/She/It is... 他/她/它是……

#### 2. 练习
填空：
1. I ___ a student. (am)
2. You ___ my friend. (are)
3. He ___ a boy. (is)
4. She ___ a girl. (is)
5. It ___ a cat. (is)
6. We ___ classmates. (are)
7. They ___ happy. (are)

### 二、人称代词

#### 1. 主格形式
- I我 you你 he他 she她 it它 we我们 they他们

#### 2. 练习
选择正确的代词：
1. ___ am a teacher. (I)
2. ___ are a student. (You)
3. ___ is my father. (He)
4. ___ is my mother. (She)
5. ___ is a dog. (It)
6. ___ are friends. (We)
7. ___ are in the classroom. (They)

### 三、简单疑问句

#### 1. Be动词疑问句
- Am I...? 我是……吗？
- Are you...? 你是……吗？
- Is he/she/it...? 他/她/它是……吗？

#### 2. 回答
- Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
- Yes, you are. / No, you aren't.
- Yes, he/she/it is. / No, he/she/it isn't.

#### 3. 练习
问句和回答配对：
1. Are you a student? → Yes, I am.
2. Is she your mother? → Yes, she is.
3. Is it a cat? → No, it isn't.
4. Are they happy? → Yes, they are.

### 四、名词复数

#### 1. 规则变化
- 一般加-s：book→books, pen→pens
- 以s, x, ch, sh结尾加-es：bus→buses, box→boxes

#### 2. 练习
写出复数形式：
1. cat → cats
2. dog → dogs
3. book → books
4. pen → pens
5. bus → buses
6. box → boxes

### 五、简单句型

#### 1. This is...
- This is a book. 这是一本书。
- This is my friend. 这是我的朋友。

#### 2. I have...
- I have a book. 我有一本书。
- I have a pen. 我有一支笔。

#### 3. 练习
造句：
1. This is ___. (a cat/a dog/a book)
2. I have ___. (a pen/a pencil/a ruler)
3. This is my ___. (friend/teacher/mother)

## 第二篇：中年级语法训练（3-4年级）

### 一、一般现在时

#### 1. 用法
- 表示经常性、习惯性的动作
- 表示客观事实

#### 2. 构成
- 肯定句：主语 + 动词原形/三单
- 否定句：主语 + don't/doesn't + 动词原形
- 疑问句：Do/Does + 主语 + 动词原形？

#### 3. 第三人称单数
- 一般加-s：play→plays, read→reads
- 以s, x, ch, sh, o结尾加-es：watch→watches, go→goes
- 辅音字母+y结尾，变y为i加-es：study→studies

#### 4. 练习
填空：
1. I ___ to school every day. (go)
2. He ___ football every weekend. (plays)
3. She ___ English very well. (speaks)
4. They ___ to the park on Sundays. (go)
5. We ___ breakfast at 7 o'clock. (have)

变成否定句：
1. I go to school by bus. → I don't go to school by bus.
2. He plays football. → He doesn't play football.
3. She speaks English. → She doesn't speak English.

变成疑问句：
1. You like apples. → Do you like apples?
2. He likes apples. → Does he like apples?
3. They go to school. → Do they go to school?

### 二、现在进行时

#### 1. 用法
- 表示此刻正在发生的动作

#### 2. 构成
- 肯定句：主语 + am/is/are + 动词ing
- 否定句：主语 + am/is/are + not + 动词ing
- 疑问句：Am/Is/Are + 主语 + 动词ing？

#### 3. 动词ing形式
- 一般直接加-ing：play→playing, read→reading
- 以不发音e结尾，去e加-ing：make→making, write→writing
- 重读闭音节，双写末尾辅音字母加-ing：run→running, swim→swimming

#### 4. 练习
填空：
1. I ___ (read) a book now. → am reading
2. He ___ (play) football now. → is playing
3. They ___ (watch) TV now. → are watching
4. She ___ (write) a letter now. → is writing
5. We ___ (have) lunch now. → are having

### 三、一般过去时

#### 1. 用法
- 表示过去发生的动作

#### 2. 构成
- 肯定句：主语 + 动词过去式
- 否定句：主语 + didn't + 动词原形
- 疑问句：Did + 主语 + 动词原形？

#### 3. 规则动词过去式
- 一般加-ed：play→played, read→read
- 以e结尾加-d：like→liked, live→lived
- 辅音字母+y结尾，变y为i加-ed：study→studied
- 重读闭音节，双写末尾辅音字母加-ed：stop→stopped

#### 4. 不规则动词过去式（常用）
- am/is→was, are→were
- go→went, have→had, do→did
- eat→ate, drink→drank, see→saw
- come→came, take→took, give→gave
- get→got, make→made, say→said
- tell→told, know→knew, think→thought

#### 5. 练习
填空：
1. I ___ (go) to the park yesterday. → went
2. He ___ (play) football last weekend. → played
3. She ___ (read) a book last night. → read
4. They ___ (watch) TV yesterday evening. → watched
5. We ___ (have) a party last week. → had

### 四、一般将来时

#### 1. 用法
- 表示将来要发生的动作

#### 2. 构成
**will结构**：
- 肯定句：主语 + will + 动词原形
- 否定句：主语 + won't + 动词原形
- 疑问句：Will + 主语 + 动词原形？

**be going to结构**：
- 肯定句：主语 + am/is/are going to + 动词原形
- 否定句：主语 + am/is/are + not going to + 动词原形
- 疑问句：Am/Is/Are + 主语 + going to + 动词原形？

#### 3. 练习
填空：
1. I ___ (go) to Beijing tomorrow. → will go / am going to go
2. He ___ (play) football this afternoon. → will play / is going to play
3. They ___ (visit) the museum next week. → will visit / are going to visit
4. She ___ (see) a movie tonight. → will see / is going to see
5. We ___ (have) a party next Saturday. → will have / are going to have

### 五、情态动词

#### 1. can
- 表示能力：I can swim. 我会游泳。
- 表示许可：Can I use your pen? 我可以用你的笔吗？

#### 2. should
- 表示建议：You should study hard. 你应该努力学习。

#### 3. must
- 表示必须：You must finish your homework. 你必须完成作业。

#### 4. 练习
填空：
1. I ___ swim very well. (can)
2. You ___ see a doctor. (should)
3. You ___ wear a uniform at school. (must)
4. ___ I borrow your book? (Can)
5. We ___ help each other. (should)

## 第三篇：高年级语法训练（5-6年级）

### 一、形容词比较级和最高级

#### 1. 比较级
- 构成：形容词 + -er 或 more + 形容词
- 用法：A + be + 比较级 + than + B

#### 2. 最高级
- 构成：the + 形容词 + -est 或 the most + 形容词
- 用法：A + be + the + 最高级 + in/of...

#### 3. 规则变化
- 单音节词加-er/-est：tall→taller→tallest
- 以e结尾加-r/-st：large→larger→largest
- 辅音字母+y结尾，变y为i加-er/-est：happy→happier→happiest
- 多音节词前加more/most：beautiful→more beautiful→most beautiful

#### 4. 不规则变化
- good/well→better→best
- bad/badly→worse→worst
- many/much→more→most
- little→less→least

#### 5. 练习
填空：
1. Tom is ___ (tall) than Jerry. → taller
2. This book is ___ (interesting) than that one. → more interesting
3. She is the ___ (beautiful) girl in our class. → most beautiful
4. He runs ___ (fast) than me. → faster
5. This is the ___ (good) movie I've ever seen. → best

### 二、定语从句

#### 1. 关系代词
- who：指人，作主语或宾语
- which：指物，作主语或宾语
- that：指人或物，作主语或宾语
- whose：指人或物，作定语

#### 2. 用法
- The boy who is playing football is my brother.
- The book which I bought yesterday is very interesting.
- The girl that I met yesterday is very nice.

#### 3. 练习
填空：
1. The boy ___ is wearing a red hat is my friend. (who/that)
2. The book ___ I read last night was very interesting. (which/that)
3. The girl ___ mother is a teacher is my classmate. (whose)
4. The car ___ he bought is very expensive. (which/that)
5. The man ___ lives next door is very friendly. (who/that)

### 三、状语从句

#### 1. 时间状语从句
- when：当……时候
- while：在……期间
- before：在……之前
- after：在……之后
- until/till：直到……

#### 2. 条件状语从句
- if：如果

#### 3. 原因状语从句
- because：因为
- since：既然
- as：由于

#### 4. 练习
填空：
1. I was doing my homework ___ my mother came home. (when)
2. ___ it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home. (If)
3. I didn't go to school ___ I was ill. (because)
4. He waited ___ the bus came. (until)
5. ___ you are tired, you should have a rest. (If/Since)

### 四、被动语态

#### 1. 构成
- 一般现在时：am/is/are + 过去分词
- 一般过去时：was/were + 过去分词
- 一般将来时：will be + 过去分词

#### 2. 用法
- 主动句：They clean the classroom every day.
- 被动句：The classroom is cleaned every day.

#### 3. 练习
改成被动语态：
1. They build houses. → Houses are built.
2. He wrote a letter. → A letter was written.
3. They will finish the work. → The work will be finished.
4. She cleans the room every day. → The room is cleaned every day.
5. He broke the window. → The window was broken.

### 五、直接引语和间接引语

#### 1. 直接引语变间接引语
- 时态变化：一般现在时→一般过去时，一般过去时→过去完成时
- 人称变化：第一人称→第三人称，第二人称→第一人称
- 时间状语变化：now→then, today→that day, yesterday→the day before

#### 2. 练习
改成间接引语：
1. He said, "I am a student." → He said that he was a student.
2. She said, "I like apples." → She said that she liked apples.
3. He said, "I went to the park yesterday." → He said that he had gone to the park the day before.
4. She said, "I will go to Beijing tomorrow." → She said that she would go to Beijing the next day.
5. He said, "I can swim." → He said that he could swim.

## 句型训练

### 一、常用句型

#### 1. 问候
- Hello! / Hi! 你好！
- Good morning! 早上好！
- How are you? 你好吗？
- I'm fine, thank you. 我很好，谢谢。

#### 2. 介绍
- My name is... 我的名字是……
- I'm from... 我来自……
- This is my... 这是我的……

#### 3. 询问
- What's your name? 你叫什么名字？
- How old are you? 你多大了？
- Where are you from? 你来自哪里？
- What do you like? 你喜欢什么？

#### 4. 表达
- I like... 我喜欢……
- I don't like... 我不喜欢……
- I want... 我想要……
- I can... 我会……

### 二、情景对话

#### 1. 购物
A: Can I help you?
B: Yes, I'd like a T-shirt, please.
A: What colour do you like?
B: I like blue.
A: Here you are.
B: How much is it?
A: It's 50 yuan.
B: I'll take it.

#### 2. 问路
A: Excuse me. Where is the library?
B: Go straight and turn left. It's on your right.
A: Is it far from here?
B: No, it's not far. You can walk there.
A: Thank you!
B: You're welcome!

#### 3. 谈论天气
A: What's the weather like today?
B: It's sunny and warm.
A: Can we go to the park?
B: Yes, let's go!
A: Great!

#### 4. 谈论爱好
A: What's your hobby?
B: I like reading books and playing basketball.
A: Do you like playing computer games?
B: Sometimes. But I don't play too much.
A: That's good!

## 学习建议

### 1. 系统学习
- 按年级循序渐进
- 理解语法规则
- 多做练习巩固

### 2. 语境运用
- 在句子中学习语法
- 多进行口语练习
- 多读英语文章

### 3. 对比记忆
- 对比不同词性的用法
- 对比不同时态的区别
- 对比相似句型的差异

### 4. 反复练习
- 每天练习语法题
- 建立错题本
- 定期复习巩固

语法是英语学习的骨架。通过系统的学习和练习，掌握语法规则，提高语言运用能力。坚持学习，多加练习，语法水平一定会有显著提高！
