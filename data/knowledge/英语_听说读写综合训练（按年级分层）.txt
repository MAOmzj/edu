小学英语听说读写综合训练（按年级分层）

本资料按年级整理英语听、说、读、写四项技能的训练内容，配听力材料、口语对话、阅读短文和写作范文。英语学习的目标是综合运用——听懂、会说、能读、善写。

============================================================
一年级
============================================================

【听力训练】
听指令做动作：
Stand up. Sit down. Touch your nose. Clap your hands.
Wave your arm. Stamp your foot. Open your mouth. Close your eyes.

听音辨物：
Listen and number.（听音标号）
cat / dog / bird / fish / duck

听对话回答问题：
A: What's this?
B: It's a cat.
Q: What's this? → It's a cat.

【口语训练】
日常问候：
Hello! Hi! Good morning! Good afternoon! Good evening!
How are you? I'm fine, thank you. And you?

自我介绍：
Hello! My name is... I'm... years old. I like...

描述物品：
What's this? It's a/an...
What color is it? It's...

简单对话：
A: Do you like apples?
B: Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

【阅读训练】
短文1：
My name is Tom. I am seven years old. I have a cat. It is white. I like it very much.
问题：What's the boy's name? → Tom. What color is the cat? → White.

短文2：
Look at my family. This is my father. He is tall. This is my mother. She is beautiful. This is me. I am short. I love my family.
问题：How many people are in the family? → Three. Is the father tall? → Yes.

【写作训练】
看图写句子：
图片：一个红苹果。→ It is a red apple.
图片：一只黄色的鸭子。→ It is a yellow duck.
图片：我有一本书。→ I have a book.

============================================================
二年级
============================================================

【听力训练】
听对话判断对错：
A: Where is the ball?
B: It's under the chair.
判断：The ball is on the chair. → False (F)

听短文填空：
I have a dog. Its name is... It is... years old. It likes to eat...

【口语训练】
描述位置：
Where is the...? It's in/on/under/behind...

谈论喜好：
What's your favorite...? My favorite... is...
Do you like...? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

日常对话：
A: What time is it?
B: It's... o'clock.

A: What day is it today?
B: It's...

A: What's the weather like?
B: It's sunny/cloudy/rainy.

【阅读训练】
短文1：
It is Sunday today. The weather is sunny. Tom and his friends go to the park. They see many flowers—red, yellow and purple. Tom flies a kite. His friends play football. They are very happy.
问题：What day is it? → Sunday. What's the weather like? → Sunny. What do they do in the park? → Tom flies a kite. His friends play football.

短文2：
My School Day
I get up at 6:30 every morning. I have breakfast at 7:00. I go to school at 7:30. We have four classes in the morning. At 12:00, I have lunch at school. In the afternoon, we have two classes. School is over at 3:30. After school, I often play basketball with my friends.
问题：What time does the writer get up? → At 6:30. How many classes in the morning? → Four. What does the writer do after school? → Play basketball with friends.

【写作训练】
我的宠物：
I have a pet... Its name is... It is... years old. It is...（颜色）. It likes to eat... I love it very much.

我的一天：
I get up at... I have breakfast at... I go to school at... I have... classes in the morning. I have lunch at... In the afternoon, I have... classes. After school, I... I go to bed at...

============================================================
三年级
============================================================

【听力训练】
听对话选择：
A: Where did you go last weekend?
B: I went to the zoo.
A: What did you see?
B: I saw monkeys, elephants and pandas.
Q: Where did B go last weekend?
A. The park  B. The zoo  C. The museum → B

听短文回答问题：
My name is Lily. I am from England. I am ten years old. I like China very much. I like Chinese food. I like to eat dumplings. I am learning Chinese now.
问题：Where is Lily from? → England. What does she like to eat? → Dumplings.

【口语训练】
谈论过去：
What did you do yesterday/last weekend?
I...（动词过去式）

描述外貌：
What does he/she look like?
He/She is tall/short/thin. He/She has...（大眼睛、长头发等）

表达建议：
What should I do?
You should...（多喝水、早点睡觉等）

情景对话：
在餐厅：
A: What would you like?
B: I'd like some noodles, please.
A: Would you like something to drink?
B: A glass of juice, please.
A: OK. Here you are.
B: Thank you.

【阅读训练】
短文1：
Last summer, I went to Beijing with my parents. We visited the Great Wall. It is very long and old. We also went to the Forbidden City. It is very big and beautiful. We ate Beijing roast duck. It was delicious. We took many photos. I had a great time in Beijing.
问题：When did the writer go to Beijing? → Last summer. Who did the writer go with? → Parents. What did they visit? → The Great Wall and the Forbidden City. What did they eat? → Beijing roast duck.

短文2：
My Best Friend
My best friend is Wang Wei. He is twelve years old. He is tall and strong. He has short black hair and bright eyes.
He is very friendly and helpful. He often helps me with my math. He is good at math and always gets good grades. We often study together after school.
He likes playing basketball very much. He plays basketball every afternoon. He is in the school basketball team. He can run very fast and jump very high.
On weekends, we often go to the park together. Sometimes we ride bikes, sometimes we fly kites. We always have a good time.
I am lucky to have such a good friend. I hope we can be friends forever.

【写作训练】
我的周末：
Last weekend, I had a wonderful time. On Saturday morning, I... In the afternoon, I... On Sunday, I... I was very happy. What a nice weekend!

给朋友的信：
Dear...,
    How are you? I miss you very much.
    Let me tell you about...（学校生活、有趣的事等）
    What about you? Please write to me soon.
    Best wishes!
    Yours,
    ...

============================================================
四至六年级
============================================================

【听力训练】
听长对话回答问题（含推理）：
M: I'd like to buy a birthday present for my sister.
W: How about a scarf? It's very popular now.
M: That's a good idea. Do you have any blue ones?
W: Yes, here you are.
M: How much is it?
W: 50 yuan.
M: OK, I'll take it.
问题：What does the man want to buy? → A birthday present for his sister. What does he buy at last? → A blue scarf. How much is it? → 50 yuan.

【口语训练】
表达观点：
What do you think about...?
I think... Because...

讨论计划：
What are you going to do this weekend?
I'm going to...

描述经历：
Tell me about your last vacation.
Last vacation, I went to... It was...

辩论讨论：
Do you think students should have homework every day?
Why or why not?

【阅读训练】
长文阅读+推理：
Thomas Edison
Thomas Edison was one of the greatest inventors in the world. He was born in America in 1847. When he was a child, he was always asking questions. He wanted to know how everything worked.
Edison invented many things in his life. His most famous invention was the light bulb. Before his invention, people used candles and oil lamps. They were not very bright. Edison worked very hard to find the right material for the light bulb. He tried thousands of times before he succeeded.
Edison once said, "Genius is one percent inspiration and ninety-nine percent perspiration." This means that hard work is more important than just having good ideas.
问题：When was Edison born? → In 1847. What was his most famous invention? → The light bulb. What did people use before the light bulb? → Candles and oil lamps. What does his famous quote mean? → Hard work is more important than just having good ideas. Do you agree with Edison? Why or why not? → (Open answer)

【写作训练】
议论文：
Should students use mobile phones at school?
Nowadays, many students have mobile phones. Should they use them at school? Different people have different opinions.
Some people think students should use phones at school. They say phones can help students learn. Students can use phones to search for information, take photos of the blackboard, and communicate with teachers.
However, other people think students should not use phones at school. They say phones can distract students from studying. Some students play games or chat on phones in class instead of listening to teachers.
In my opinion, students should not use phones at school. School is a place for studying. Students should focus on their lessons. If they need to use phones for learning, teachers can arrange specific time for that.
In conclusion, I think schools should make rules to limit phone use. This will help students study better.

英语学习的四项技能——听说读写——就像桌子的四条腿，缺一不可。每天坚持听10分钟英语、说5个句子、读1篇短文、写3-5句话，日积月累，英语水平就会稳步提升。记住：不怕慢，就怕站——每天进步一点点，终将到达成功的彼岸。
