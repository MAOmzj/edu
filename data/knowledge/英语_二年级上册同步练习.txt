# 英语二年级上册同步练习

## Unit 1: My Friends

### 一、核心词汇

#### 描述人物
- tall 高的
- short 矮的
- thin 瘦的
- fat 胖的
- long 长的
- big 大的
- small 小的

#### 外貌特征
- hair 头发
- eye 眼睛
- ear 耳朵
- nose 鼻子
- mouth 嘴巴
- face 脸

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述外貌
- He/She is... 他/她是……
  例：He is tall. 他很高。
- He/She has... 他/她有……
  例：She has long hair. 她有长头发。

#### 2. 询问外貌
- What does he/she look like? 他/她长什么样？
- He/She is... and has... 他/她是……并且有……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听描述，选择正确的图片：
1. She has long hair. A. 👩‍🦰 B. 👩‍🦱
2. He is tall. A. 🧑‍🦰 B. 🧒

#### 口语练习
描述你的好朋友：
- My friend is Tom.
- He is tall and thin.
- He has short hair and big eyes.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：tall, short, thin, fat, long, big, small

### 四、对话

#### 介绍朋友
A: Who is that boy?
B: He's my friend, Mike.
A: What does he look like?
B: He's tall and has short hair.
A: He looks nice!

## Unit 2: In the Park

### 一、核心词汇

#### 公园设施
- park 公园
- tree 树
- flower 花
- grass 草
- lake 湖
- bridge 桥
- bench 长椅
- path 小路

#### 活动
- walk 散步
- run 跑步
- play 玩耍
- sit 坐
- fly a kite 放风筝

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述位置
- There is/are... 有……
  例：There is a lake in the park. 公园里有一个湖。

#### 2. 询问位置
- Where is the...? ……在哪里？
- It's near/behind/in front of... 它在……附近/后面/前面

#### 3. 描述活动
- What are you doing? 你在做什么？
- I'm...ing. 我正在……
  例：I'm walking. 我正在散步。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的地点：
1. There is a bridge. A. 🌉 B. 🌳
2. There are flowers. A. 🌸 B. 🌊

#### 口语练习
描述公园：
- There are many trees in the park.
- There is a big lake.
- People are walking and playing.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：park, tree, flower, grass, lake, bridge

### 四、歌曲

#### 《Walking in the Park》
Walking, walking in the park,
Walking, walking in the park.
I can see the trees so green,
The prettiest park I've ever seen.

## Unit 3: At the Zoo

### 一、核心词汇

#### 动物（复习）
- lion 狮子
- tiger 老虎
- bear 熊
- elephant 大象
- monkey 猴子
- panda 熊猫
- zebra 斑马
- giraffe 长颈鹿

#### 描述动物
- strong 强壮的
- cute 可爱的
- scary 可怕的
- friendly 友好的

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述动物
- The... is... ……是……
  例：The elephant is big. 大象很大。
- The... has... ……有……
  例：The giraffe has a long neck. 长颈鹿有长脖子。

#### 2. 表达喜好
- I like... because... 我喜欢……因为……
  例：I like pandas because they're cute. 我喜欢熊猫因为它们很可爱。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听描述，猜动物：
1. It's big and has a long nose. It's an elephant.
2. It's black and white. It's a panda.

#### 口语练习
描述你最喜欢的动物：
- My favorite animal is the lion.
- It's strong and scary.
- It has a big mouth.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：strong, cute, scary, friendly

### 四、对话

#### 在动物园
A: Look at the monkeys!
B: They're so cute!
A: What's your favorite animal?
B: I like elephants. They're big and strong.
A: Me too! Let's go see them.

## Unit 4: My Schoolbag

### 一、核心词汇

#### 学校用品
- schoolbag 书包
- book 书
- notebook 笔记本
- pencil 铅笔
- pen 钢笔
- ruler 尺子
- eraser 橡皮
- crayon 蜡笔

#### 颜色（复习）
- red 红色
- blue 蓝色
- yellow 黄色
- green 绿色
- pink 粉色
- purple 紫色

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问物品
- What's in your schoolbag? 你书包里有什么？
- There is/are... 有……
  例：There are three books. 有三本书。

#### 2. 询问颜色
- What color is it? 它是什么颜色？
- It's... 它是……色的

#### 3. 询问数量
- How many...? 有多少……？
- There are... 有……
  例：How many pencils? There are five.

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的物品：
1. There is a red book. A. 📕 B. 📘
2. There are three pencils. A. ✏️✏️✏️ B. ✏️✏️

#### 口语练习
描述你的书包：
- I have a blue schoolbag.
- There are four books in it.
- There are two pencils and one eraser.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：schoolbag, book, notebook, pencil, pen, ruler

### 四、游戏

#### 猜物品
A: I have something in my schoolbag.
B: What is it?
A: It's red and long.
B: Is it a ruler?
A: Yes, it is!

## Unit 5: Food I Like

### 一、核心词汇

#### 食物（复习扩展）
- apple 苹果
- banana 香蕉
- orange 橙子
- grape 葡萄
- strawberry 草莓
- watermelon 西瓜
- bread 面包
- cake 蛋糕
- cookie 饼干
- candy 糖果

#### 味道
- sweet 甜的
- sour 酸的
- yummy 好吃的

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 表达喜好
- I like... 我喜欢……
- I don't like... 我不喜欢……
- My favorite food is... 我最喜欢的食物是……

#### 2. 询问喜好
- Do you like...? 你喜欢……吗？
- Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

#### 3. 描述味道
- It's sweet/sour/yummy. 它是甜的/酸的/好吃的。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的食物：
1. I like apples. They're sweet. A. 🍎 B. 🍌
2. I don't like grapes. They're sour. A. 🍇 B. 🍊

#### 口语练习
描述你喜欢的食物：
- I like strawberries.
- They're sweet and yummy.
- My favorite fruit is watermelon.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：grape, strawberry, watermelon, cookie, candy, sweet, sour

### 四、歌曲

#### 《Apples and Bananas》
Apples and bananas, pears and plums,
I eat them all, I eat them all.
Apples and bananas, pears and plums,
I eat them all, I eat them all.

## Unit 6: Seasons

### 一、核心词汇

#### 季节
- spring 春天
- summer 夏天
- autumn/fall 秋天
- winter 冬天

#### 天气
- warm 温暖的
- hot 热的
- cool 凉爽的
- cold 冷的

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述季节
- In spring, it's warm. 春天，天气温暖。
- I like... because... 我喜欢……因为……
  例：I like summer because I can swim. 我喜欢夏天因为我可以游泳。

#### 2. 询问喜欢的季节
- What's your favorite season? 你最喜欢哪个季节？
- My favorite season is... 我最喜欢的季节是……

#### 3. 描述活动
- In..., I can... 在……，我可以……
  例：In winter, I can make a snowman. 在冬天，我可以堆雪人。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的季节：
1. It's hot. I can swim. A. ☀️ summer B. ❄️ winter
2. It's cold. I can make a snowman. A. 🌸 spring B. ⛄ winter

#### 口语练习
描述你喜欢的季节：
- My favorite season is spring.
- It's warm and beautiful.
- The flowers are blooming.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：spring, summer, autumn, winter, warm, hot, cool, cold

### 四、歌曲

#### 《Seasons Song》
Spring is green, summer is bright,
Autumn is yellow, winter is white.
Spring is green, summer is bright,
Autumn is yellow, winter is white.

## Unit 7: My Day

### 一、核心词汇

#### 时间
- morning 早上
- afternoon 下午
- evening 晚上
- night 晚上
- today 今天
- tomorrow 明天
- yesterday 昨天

#### 日常活动
- get up 起床
- have breakfast 吃早餐
- go to school 上学
- have lunch 吃午餐
- go home 回家
- have dinner 吃晚餐
- do homework 做作业
- go to bed 上床睡觉

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述日常
- I...in the morning. 我早上……
  例：I get up at 7 o'clock in the morning. 我早上7点起床。
- I...in the afternoon. 我下午……
- I...in the evening. 我晚上……

#### 2. 询问时间
- What time do you...? 你什么时候……？
- I...at... 我在……点……
  例：What time do you get up? I get up at 7.

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的时间：
1. I get up at 7 o'clock. A. 7:00 B. 8:00
2. I go to bed at 9 o'clock. A. 9:00 B. 10:00

#### 口语练习
描述你的一天：
- I get up at 7 o'clock in the morning.
- I have breakfast at 7:30.
- I go to school at 8 o'clock.
- I have lunch at 12 o'clock.
- I go home at 4 o'clock in the afternoon.
- I have dinner at 6 o'clock in the evening.
- I go to bed at 9 o'clock at night.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：get up, have breakfast, go to school, have lunch, go home, have dinner, do homework, go to bed

### 四、对话

#### 谈论日常
A: What time do you get up?
B: I get up at 7 o'clock.
A: What do you do in the morning?
B: I have breakfast and go to school.
A: What about in the afternoon?
B: I go home at 4 o'clock and do homework.

## Unit 8: Holidays

### 一、核心词汇

#### 节日
- Spring Festival 春节
- Mid-Autumn Festival 中秋节
- Christmas 圣诞节
- Halloween 万圣节
- Children's Day 儿童节
- Teacher's Day 教师节

#### 节日活动
- eat dumplings 吃饺子
- eat mooncakes 吃月饼
- give presents 送礼物
- dress up 装扮
- play games 玩游戏

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 介绍节日
- ... is a... holiday. ……是一个……节日。
  例：Spring Festival is a Chinese holiday. 春节是一个中国节日。

#### 2. 描述活动
- We...on... 我们在…………
  例：We eat dumplings on Spring Festival. 我们在春节吃饺子。

#### 3. 表达喜好
- I like... 我喜欢……
- My favorite holiday is... 我最喜欢的节日是……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的节日：
1. We eat mooncakes. A. 🥮 Mid-Autumn Festival B. 🎄 Christmas
2. We give presents. A. 🎁 Christmas B. 🏮 Spring Festival

#### 口语练习
介绍你喜欢的节日：
- My favorite holiday is Spring Festival.
- We eat dumplings and give red envelopes.
- I like it because I can get lucky money.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：Spring Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival, Christmas, Halloween

### 四、歌曲
