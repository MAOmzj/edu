小学英语分级阅读训练（按年级分层）

本资料按低、中、高三个年级段，提供分级英语阅读训练材料。每篇配生词注释、阅读理解和答案。坚持分级阅读，英语水平会稳步提升。

============================================================
第一篇：Level 1（1-2年级）简单句子与短文
============================================================

Passage 1: My Pet
I have a pet cat. Her name is Mimi. She is white and soft. She likes to eat fish. She sleeps on my bed. I love Mimi.

Words: pet(宠物), soft(柔软的), bed(床)

Questions:
1. What pet does the writer have? → A cat.
2. What is the cat's name? → Mimi.
3. What color is the cat? → White.
4. What does the cat like to eat? → Fish.

Passage 2: My Family
There are four people in my family: my father, my mother, my sister and me. My father is a doctor. My mother is a teacher. My sister is five years old. I am ten years old. We are happy.

Words: family(家庭), doctor(医生), teacher(老师)

Questions:
1. How many people are in the family? → Four.
2. What does the father do? → He is a doctor.
3. What does the mother do? → She is a teacher.
4. How old is the sister? → Five years old.

Passage 3: At the Park
It is Sunday. The weather is sunny. Tom and his friends go to the park. They see many flowers—red, yellow and purple. Tom flies a kite. His friends play football. They are very happy.

Words: weather(天气), sunny(晴朗的), flower(花), kite(风筝)

Questions:
1. What day is it? → Sunday.
2. What's the weather like? → Sunny.
3. Where do they go? → To the park.
4. What does Tom do? → He flies a kite.

============================================================
第二篇：Level 2（3-4年级）短文与对话
============================================================

Passage 4: A Rainy Day
It was raining hard on Saturday morning. Lily wanted to go to the library, but she couldn't go out in the rain. So she stayed at home and read books. Her mother made hot chocolate for her. Lily sat by the window and read her favorite book. The rain stopped in the afternoon. Lily went to the library with her mother. She borrowed three books. It was a nice day after all.

Words: hard(猛烈地), library(图书馆), hot chocolate(热巧克力), borrow(借)

Questions:
1. What was the weather like on Saturday morning? → It was raining hard.
2. Why couldn't Lily go to the library in the morning? → Because it was raining hard.
3. What did Lily do at home? → She read books.
4. How many books did Lily borrow? → Three books.

Passage 5: My School Day
I get up at 6:30 every morning. I have breakfast at 7:00. I go to school at 7:30. We have four classes in the morning. At 12:00, I have lunch at school. In the afternoon, we have two classes. School is over at 3:30. After school, I often play basketball with my friends. I go home at 5:00. I do my homework in the evening. I go to bed at 9:00.

Words: get up(起床), breakfast(早餐), class(课), afternoon(下午)

Questions:
1. What time does the writer get up? → At 6:30.
2. How many classes in the morning? → Four.
3. Where does the writer have lunch? → At school.
4. What does the writer do after school? → Play basketball with friends.

第三篇：Level 3（5-6年级）长文与推理
============================================================

Passage 6: The Ant and the Dove
One hot day, an ant was looking for some water. He fell into a river and cried for help. A dove saw him and dropped a leaf into the water. The ant climbed onto the leaf and was saved. A few days later, the ant saw a hunter aiming at the dove. The ant quickly bit the hunter's foot. The hunter cried out and missed the shot. The dove flew away safely.

Words: ant(蚂蚁), dove(鸽子), river(河), leaf(树叶), saved(得救), hunter(猎人), aim(瞄准), bite(咬), miss(错过)

Questions:
1. Why did the ant fall into the river? → He was looking for water and fell in.
2. How did the dove help the ant? → She dropped a leaf into the water.
3. How did the ant help the dove? → He bit the hunter's foot.
4. What can we learn from the story? → Friends should help each other. / Good deeds are rewarded.

Passage 7: Thomas Edison
Thomas Edison was one of the greatest inventors in the world. He was born in America in 1847. When he was a child, he was always asking questions. He wanted to know how everything worked.
Edison invented many things in his life. His most famous invention was the light bulb. Before his invention, people used candles and oil lamps. They were not very bright. Edison worked very hard to find the right material for the light bulb. He tried thousands of times before he succeeded.
Edison once said, "Genius is one percent inspiration and ninety-nine percent perspiration." This means that hard work is more important than just having good ideas.

Words: inventor(发明家), invent(发明), light bulb(灯泡), material(材料), succeed(成功), genius(天才), inspiration(灵感), perspiration(汗水)

Questions:
1. When was Edison born? → In 1847.
2. What was his most famous invention? → The light bulb.
3. What did people use before the light bulb? → Candles and oil lamps.
4. What does his famous quote mean? → Hard work is more important than just having good ideas.
5. Do you agree with Edison? Why or why not? → (Open answer) Yes, because even if you have a good idea, you still need to work hard to make it come true.

============================================================
第四篇：阅读技巧总结
============================================================

【Level 1 技巧】
找关键词：在文中找到题目涉及的词。
直接回答：答案通常就在原文中。
用完整句子回答。

【Level 2 技巧】
先读题目，再读文章——带着问题阅读。
注意时间顺序和因果关系。
推断词义：根据上下文猜生词意思。

【Level 3 技巧】
通读全文，把握主旨。
注意段落之间的逻辑关系。
区分事实和观点。
开放题要有理有据。

分级阅读是提升英语水平的最佳途径。每天读一篇适合自己水平的英语短文，积累词汇，培养语感。不要贪多——读懂比读多更重要。从Level 1开始，逐步提升到Level 3，你会发现英语阅读越来越轻松！
