# 英语六年级上册核心知识与练习

## Unit 1: How can I get there?

### 核心词汇
- science museum 科学博物馆
- post office 邮局
- bookstore 书店
- cinema 电影院
- hospital 医院
- crossing 十字路口
- restaurant 餐馆
- turn 转弯
- left 左
- right 右
- straight 直行

### 核心句型
- Where is the...? ……在哪里？
- How can I get to...? 我怎样到达……？
- Turn left/right at... 在……向左/右转。
- Go straight. 直走。
- It's next to/near/behind... 它在……旁边/附近/后面。

### 重点练习
问路和指路。

### 情景对话示例
A: Excuse me. Where is the cinema?
B: Go straight. Then turn right at the crossing. It's on the left.
A: Thank you!
B: You're welcome.

## Unit 2: Ways to go to school

### 核心词汇
- on foot 步行
- by bus 乘公共汽车
- by plane 乘飞机
- by taxi 乘出租车
- by ship 乘轮船
- by subway 乘地铁
- by train 乘火车
- by bike 骑自行车
- slow 慢的
- fast 快的

### 核心句型
- How do you come to school? 你怎么来学校？
- I often/usually come to school by... 我经常/通常……来学校。
- How does he/she go to...? 他/她怎样去……？
- He/She goes to...by... 他/她……去……。

### 重点练习
询问和描述交通方式。

### 情景对话示例
A: How do you come to school?
B: I usually come to school by bus. Sometimes I come on foot.
A: How does your father go to work?
B: He goes to work by car.

## Unit 3: My weekend plan

### 核心词汇
- visit 拜访
- film 电影
- see a film 看电影
- trip 旅行
- take a trip 去旅行
- supermarket 超市
- evening 晚上
- tonight 今晚
- tomorrow 明天
- next week 下周
- dictionary 字典
- comic book 连环画
- word 单词
- lesson 课

### 核心句型
- What are you going to do...? 你……打算做什么？
- I'm going to... 我打算……
- Where are you going? 你打算去哪里？
- I'm going to the... 我打算去……
- When are you going? 你打算什么时候去？
- I'm going to...this morning/afternoon/evening. 我今天上午/下午/晚上打算……

### 重点练习
谈论计划和打算（一般将来时）。

### 情景对话示例
A: What are you going to do tomorrow?
B: I'm going to see a film.
A: Where are you going?
B: I'm going to the cinema.
A: When are you going?
B: I'm going tomorrow evening.

## Unit 4: I have a pen pal

### 核心词汇
- study 学习
- puzzle 谜
- hiking 远足
- hobby 爱好
- jasmine 茉莉
- idea 主意
- pen pal 笔友

### 核心句型
- What are your hobbies? 你的爱好是什么？
- I like... 我喜欢……
- Do you like...? 你喜欢……吗？
- Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- Does he/she like...? 他/她喜欢……吗？
- Yes, he/she does. / No, he/she doesn't.
- He/She likes... 他/她喜欢……

### 重点练习
询问和描述爱好（第三人称单数）。

### 情景对话示例
A: What are your hobbies?
B: I like reading stories and playing football.
A: Does your pen pal like hiking?
B: Yes, he does. He also likes studying Chinese.

## Unit 5: What does he do?

### 核心词汇
- factory 工厂
- worker 工人
- postman 邮递员
- businessman 商人
- police officer 警察
- fisherman 渔民
- scientist 科学家
- pilot 飞行员
- coach 教练
- country 国家
- head 头
- stay 保持
- untill 直到
- athlete 运动员

### 核心句型
- What does he/she do? 他/她是做什么的？
- He/She is a... 他/她是一个……
- Where does he/she work? 他/她在哪里工作？
- He/She works in/at... 他/她在……工作。
- How does he/she go to work? 他/她怎样去上班？
- He/She goes to work by... 他/她……去上班。

### 重点练习
询问和描述职业及工作地点。

### 情景对话示例
A: What does your father do?
B: He's a police officer.
A: Where does he work?
B: He works at the police station.
A: How does he go to work?
B: He goes to work by car.

## Unit 6: How do you feel?

### 核心词汇
- angry 生气的
- afraid 害怕的
- sad 难过的
- worried 担心的
- happy 高兴的
- see a doctor 看病
- wear 穿
- more 更多的
- deep 深的
- breath 呼吸
- count 数数

### 核心句型
- How do you feel? 你感觉怎样？
- I'm... 我……
- What's wrong? 怎么了？
- I'm... 我……
- You should... 你应该……
- Don't be... 不要……

### 重点练习
表达感受和给出建议。

### 情景对话示例
A: How do you feel?
B: I'm sad.
A: What's wrong?
B: My cat is ill.
A: Don't be sad. It will be OK.
B: Thank you.

## 期末复习重点

### 词汇分类
1. 地点：science museum, post office, bookstore, cinema, hospital
2. 方向：left, right, straight, crossing, turn
3. 交通方式：on foot, by bus, by plane, by taxi, by subway
4. 时间：tonight, tomorrow, next week, this evening
5. 爱好：reading, hiking, studying, playing
6. 职业：factory worker, postman, businessman, police officer
7. 情感：angry, afraid, sad, worried, happy

### 句型总结
1. 问路：Where is the...? How can I get to...?
2. 交通方式：How do you come to school? I...by...
3. 计划：What are you going to do? I'm going to...
4. 爱好：What are your hobbies? I like... Does he/she like...?
5. 职业：What does he/she do? He/She is a...
6. 感受：How do you feel? I'm...

### 语法重点
1. 一般将来时：be going to + 动词原形
2. 第三人称单数：动词加s/es，助动词用does
3. 特殊疑问词：Where, How, What, When
4. 交通方式：by + 交通工具
5. 情态动词should的用法

### 情景对话
1. 问路和指路
2. 谈论交通方式
3. 讨论周末计划
4. 介绍笔友和爱好
5. 谈论职业
6. 表达感受和给建议

## 学习建议
- 熟记地点和方向词汇
- 掌握一般将来时的用法
- 注意第三人称单数的变化
- 多进行情景对话练习
- 学会用英语表达感受和建议
