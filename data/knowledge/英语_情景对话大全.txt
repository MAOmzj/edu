小学英语情景对话大全百科

本资料面向小学三至六年级，按场景系统整理日常英语口语对话。每个场景配核心句型、完整对话和实用表达。熟读对话、模仿语音语调，口语就能脱口而出。

============================================================
第一篇：校园生活
============================================================

一、课堂用语

Teacher: Good morning, class!
Students: Good morning, Miss Li!
Teacher: Sit down, please. Open your books, please. Turn to page twenty.
Teacher: Who can answer this question?
Student: Let me try! / I can!
Teacher: Very good! / Excellent! / Well done!
Student: Thank you, teacher.
Teacher: Don't be shy. Try again.
Student: May I ask a question?
Teacher: Of course. Go ahead.
Student: I don't understand. Can you say it again?
Teacher: Read after me. Listen carefully.
Teacher: Class is over. Goodbye, everyone!
Students: Goodbye, teacher!

二、课间活动

A: What do you want to do during the break?
B: Let's play basketball!
A: Good idea! Do you want to join us, Tom?
C: Sorry, I need to finish my homework first.
A: OK. Let's go to the playground!
B: Wait for me! I'll get my basketball.
（在操场上）
A: Pass me the ball!
B: Here you are! Catch!
A: Great shot!
B: Thank you! Let's play again after school.

三、借东西

A: Excuse me, may I borrow your ruler?
B: Sure. Here you are.
A: Thank you very much.
B: You're welcome. Please give it back after class.
A: I will. Thanks!

A: Do you have an eraser? I forgot mine.
B: Yes, I do. Here you are.
A: Thanks a lot!
B: No problem.

第二篇：家庭生活
============================================================

四、起床与早餐

Mum: Wake up, Tom! It's seven o'clock. Time to get up!
Tom: OK, Mum. Just five more minutes...
Mum: No! You'll be late for school. Brush your teeth and wash your face.
Tom: What's for breakfast?
Mum: Bread, eggs and milk.
Tom: I don't want eggs. Can I have some noodles?
Mum: Sorry, we don't have noodles today. Eat your eggs. They're good for you.
Tom: OK. The bread is delicious, Mum!
Mum: Eat more. Drink your milk, too.
Tom: I'm full. Thank you, Mum!
Mum: Put on your coat. It's cold outside. Have a good day at school!
Tom: Bye, Mum!

五、做家务

Mum: Tom, can you help me with the housework?
Tom: Sure. What can I do?
Mum: Please sweep the floor.
Tom: OK. Is that all?
Mum: No. After that, please clean your room. Your desk is very messy.
Tom: Alright. Can I watch TV after I finish?
Mum: Yes, but first finish your homework.
Tom: Deal!

六、购物

（在超市）
Mum: What do we need?
Tom: We need some milk, bread, and eggs.
Mum: OK. Let's go to the dairy section first.
Tom: Mum, can I have some ice cream?
Mum: Let me see... The price is fifteen yuan. OK, but just one.
Tom: Thank you, Mum! You're the best!
（在收银台）
Cashier: That's sixty-eight yuan in total.
Mum: Here's one hundred yuan.
Cashier: Here's your change—thirty-two yuan. Thank you!
Mum: Thank you. Let's go home.

第三篇：社交场景
============================================================

七、初次见面

A: Hello! My name is Amy. What's your name?
B: Hi! I'm Li Ming. Nice to meet you!
A: Nice to meet you, too! Where are you from?
B: I'm from Beijing. What about you?
A: I'm from Shanghai. How old are you?
B: I'm eleven. And you?
A: I'm eleven, too! We're the same age!
B: What class are you in?
A: I'm in Class Two, Grade Five.
B: Great! Let's be friends!
A: Sure! Here's my phone number. Call me this weekend!

八、邀请与做客

（电话邀请）
A: Hello, may I speak to Wang Wei?
B: This is Wang Wei speaking. Who's that?
A: This is Li Ming. Are you free this Saturday?
B: Yes, I think so. Why?
A: I'm having a birthday party. Would you like to come?
B: I'd love to! What time?
A: At two o'clock in the afternoon.
B: Great! See you on Saturday!
A: See you!

（做客）
A: Welcome to my home! Come in, please.
B: Thank you! Happy birthday! This is for you.
A: Oh, a book! Thank you so much!
B: You're welcome.
A: Would you like something to drink?
B: A glass of juice, please.
A: Here you are. Help yourself to some cake.
B: It's delicious!
A: Let me show you my room.
B: Wow, your room is so nice! You have so many books!
A: I like reading. Do you want to read together?
B: That sounds great!

九、问路

A: Excuse me, where is the hospital?
B: Go straight along this road. Turn right at the second crossing. You'll see it on your left.
A: Is it far from here?
B: It's about ten minutes on foot.
A: Can I take a bus?
B: Yes, you can take the No. 5 bus. Get off at the hospital stop.
A: Thank you very much!
B: You're welcome.

A: Excuse me, how can I get to the park?
B: Sorry, I don't know. I'm new here. You can ask the policeman over there.
A: OK. Thank you anyway.

第四篇：公共场所
============================================================

十、在餐厅

Waiter: Welcome! How many people?
A: Three, please.
Waiter: This way, please. Here's the menu.
A: Thank you. What would you like?
B: I'd like some noodles. What about you?
A: I'll have a hamburger and some French fries.
Waiter: Would you like something to drink?
A: Three glasses of orange juice, please.
Waiter: OK. Anything else?
B: No, that's all. Thank you.
（用餐后）
A: Waiter, bill please.
Waiter: That's one hundred and twenty yuan.
A: Here you are.
Waiter: Thank you. Have a nice day!

十一、在医院

Doctor: What's the matter?
Patient: I have a headache and a sore throat.
Doctor: Let me see. Open your mouth and say "Ah".
Patient: Ah...
Doctor: You have a cold. Do you have a fever?
Patient: Yes, I feel a little hot.
Doctor: Let me take your temperature... You have a low fever. Take this medicine three times a day. Drink more water and get enough rest.
Patient: Thank you, doctor.
Doctor: You're welcome. Get well soon!

十二、在图书馆

Librarian: Shh! Please keep quiet in the library.
A: Sorry. May I borrow this book?
Librarian: Of course. Here's your library card. You can keep it for two weeks.
A: How many books can I borrow at a time?
Librarian: You can borrow up to five books.
A: Thank you!
Librarian: Remember to return it on time.

第五篇：电话用语
============================================================

十三、打电话

A: Hello! May I speak to Zhang Wei?
B: This is Zhang Wei speaking. Who's calling?
A: Hi, Zhang Wei. This is Li Hua. Are you free tomorrow?
B: Let me think... Yes, I'm free. What's up?
A: Would you like to go to the museum with me?
B: That sounds great! What time shall we meet?
A: How about nine o'clock in the morning?
B: OK. Where shall we meet?
A: At the school gate.
B: All right. See you tomorrow!
A: See you!

十四、留言

A: Hello! May I speak to Amy?
B: Sorry, she's not in. Can I take a message?
A: Yes, please. Tell her I called. This is Tom.
B: OK, Tom. Anything else?
A: Please tell her to call me back. My number is 12345678.
B: Sure. I'll give her the message when she comes back.
A: Thank you!

第六篇：节日与天气
============================================================

十五、谈论天气

A: What's the weather like today?
B: It's sunny and warm. A beautiful day!
A: What about tomorrow?
B: The weather forecast says it will be cloudy with a chance of rain.
A: Oh, I hope it doesn't rain. We're having a picnic tomorrow.
B: Don't worry. It might be fine.
A: What's the temperature?
B: It's about twenty-five degrees.
A: That's perfect for outdoor activities!

十六、节日祝福

A: Merry Christmas!
B: Merry Christmas! Happy New Year!
A: How do you celebrate Spring Festival?
B: We have a big family dinner, watch TV, set off fireworks, and children get red envelopes.
A: That sounds wonderful! What's your favourite festival?
B: I like Children's Day best. We don't have school and we can play all day!
A: Me too!

口语练习的秘诀：多听、多模仿、多开口。不要怕犯错——说错了就改，改了就进步。每天花10分钟读对话、模仿录音的语音语调，一个月后你就会发现口语有明显进步。
