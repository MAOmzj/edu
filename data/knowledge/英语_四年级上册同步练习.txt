# 英语四年级上册同步练习

## Unit 1: My classroom

### 一、核心词汇

#### 教室物品
- classroom 教室
- window 窗户
- blackboard 黑板
- light 电灯
- picture 图画
- door 门
- teacher's desk 讲台
- computer 计算机
- fan 风扇
- wall 墙壁

#### 物品位置
- near 在……附近
- under 在……下面
- in 在……里面

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问物品位置
- Where is it? 它在哪里？
- It's in/on/under/near... 它在……里面/上面/下面/附近。

#### 2. 描述教室
- This is my classroom. 这是我的教室。
- It's big and clean. 它又大又干净。

#### 3. 提出建议
- Let's clean the classroom! 让我们打扫教室吧！
- Let me help you. 让我来帮助你。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的图片：
1. The book is on the desk. A. 📚 B. 📖
2. The ball is under the chair. A. ⚽ B. 🎈

#### 口语练习
描述你的教室：
- This is my classroom.
- It's big and clean.
- There is a blackboard on the wall.
- There are many desks and chairs.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：classroom, window, blackboard, light, picture, door

### 四、对话

#### 打扫教室
A: Let's clean the classroom!
B: OK! Where is the broom?
A: It's near the door.
B: Let me help you.
A: Thank you!

## Unit 2: My schoolbag

### 一、核心词汇

#### 学习用品
- schoolbag 书包
- English book 英语书
- Chinese book 语文书
- maths book 数学书
- storybook 故事书
- notebook 笔记本
- pencil box 铅笔盒
- key 钥匙
- toy 玩具

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问书包里的物品
- What's in your schoolbag? 你书包里有什么？
- An English book, a maths book and... 一本英语书，一本数学书和……

#### 2. 询问物品归属
- Is this your...? 这是你的……吗？
- Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的物品：
1. I have a maths book. A. 📗 B. 📘
2. Where is my pencil box? A. ✏️ B. 🎒

#### 口语练习
描述你的书包：
- I have a schoolbag.
- It's blue.
- There is an English book, a maths book and a pencil box in it.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：schoolbag, English book, Chinese book, maths book, storybook

### 四、对话

#### 找东西
A: Where is my schoolbag?
B: Is it on the desk?
A: No, it isn't.
B: Is it under the chair?
A: Yes, it is! Thank you!
B: You're welcome!

## Unit 3: My friends

### 一、核心词汇

#### 外貌特征
- tall 高的
- short 矮的
- thin 瘦的
- strong 强壮的
- friendly 友好的
- quiet 安静的
- active 活跃的

#### 身体部位
- hair 头发
- eye 眼睛
- glasses 眼镜
- shoe 鞋子

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述朋友
- He/She is... 他/她是……
  例：He is tall and strong. 他又高又壮。
- He/She has... 他/她有……
  例：She has long hair. 她有长头发。

#### 2. 猜测身份
- Who is he/she? 他/她是谁？
- His/Her name is... 他/她的名字是……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听描述，选择正确的人物：
1. He is tall and strong. He has short hair. A. 👨 B. 👦
2. She is quiet. She has long hair. A. 👩 B. 👧

#### 口语练习
描述你的好朋友：
- My best friend is Tom.
- He is tall and thin.
- He has short hair and big eyes.
- He is friendly and active.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：tall, short, thin, strong, friendly, quiet, hair, eye, glasses

### 四、对话

#### 介绍朋友
A: Who is your best friend?
B: My best friend is Amy.
A: What does she look like?
B: She is tall and thin. She has long hair.
A: Is she friendly?
B: Yes, she is. She is very friendly and quiet.

## Unit 4: My home

### 一、核心词汇

#### 房间
- bedroom 卧室
- living room 客厅
- kitchen 厨房
- bathroom 浴室
- study 书房
- phone 电话
- table 桌子
- sofa 沙发
- fridge 冰箱

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述房间
- This is my... 这是我的……
  例：This is my bedroom. 这是我的卧室。
- It's nice/clean/big... 它很……

#### 2. 询问位置
- Where is...? ……在哪里？
- It's in/on/under... 它在……里面/上面/下面。

#### 3. 请求许可
- Can I...? 我可以……吗？
- Sure. / No, you can't.

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的房间：
1. This is my bedroom. A. 🛏️ B. 🛋️
2. Where is the phone? It's on the table. A. 📱 B. ☎️

#### 口语练习
描述你的家：
- This is my home.
- There is a living room, a kitchen, a bathroom and two bedrooms.
- My bedroom is small but clean.
- There is a bed, a desk and a chair in my bedroom.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：bedroom, living room, kitchen, bathroom, study, phone, table

### 四、对话

#### 参观新家
A: Welcome to my new home!
B: Wow! It's so nice!
A: This is the living room.
B: It's big and clean. Where is your bedroom?
A: It's next to the bathroom.
B: Can I have a look?
A: Sure. This way, please.

## Unit 5: Dinner's ready

### 一、核心词汇

#### 食物
- beef 牛肉
- chicken 鸡肉
- noodles 面条
- soup 汤
- vegetable 蔬菜
- chopsticks 筷子
- bowl 碗
- fork 餐叉
- knife 刀子
- spoon 勺子

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 表达想要
- I'd like some..., please. 我想要一些……
  例：I'd like some noodles, please. 我想要一些面条。

#### 2. 提供帮助
- What would you like? 你想要什么？
- Help yourself. 请自便。

#### 3. 询问物品
- Would you like...? 你想要……吗？
- Yes, please. / No, thanks.

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的食物：
1. I'd like some beef. A. 🥩 B. 🍗
2. Would you like some soup? A. 🍲 B. 🍜

#### 口语练习
模拟点餐：
- I'd like some noodles and chicken, please.
- Would you like some soup?
- Yes, please. Thank you!

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：beef, chicken, noodles, soup, vegetable, chopsticks, bowl

### 四、对话

#### 在餐厅
A: Dinner's ready! Help yourself.
B: Thank you! What's for dinner?
A: We have beef, chicken, noodles and soup.
B: I'd like some noodles and chicken, please.
A: Would you like some soup?
B: Yes, please. Thank you!
A: You're welcome!

## Unit 6: Meet my family

### 一、核心词汇

#### 家庭成员
- parents 父母
- uncle 叔叔/舅舅
- aunt 阿姨/姑姑
- cousin 堂/表兄弟姐妹
- baby brother 小弟弟

#### 职业
- doctor 医生
- nurse 护士
- driver 司机
- farmer 农民
- cook 厨师

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 介绍家人
- This is my... 这是我的……
  例：This is my father. 这是我的爸爸。
- He's/She's a... 他/她是一个……
  例：He's a doctor. 他是医生。

#### 2. 询问身份
- Who is he/she? 他/她是谁？
- He's/She's my... 他/她是我……

#### 3. 询问职业
- What's your father/mother's job? 你爸爸/妈妈是做什么的？
- He's/She's a... 他/她是一个……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的职业：
1. He's a doctor. A. 👨‍⚕️ B. 👨‍🏫
2. She's a nurse. A. 👩‍⚕️ B. 👩‍🍳

#### 口语练习
介绍你的家人：
- This is my family.
- This is my father. He's tall and strong. He's a driver.
- This is my mother. She's beautiful. She's a nurse.
- I love my family.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：parents, uncle, aunt, cousin, doctor, nurse, driver, farmer

### 四、对话

#### 介绍全家福
A: Look! This is my family photo.
B: Who is that man?
A: He's my father. He's a doctor.
B: Who is that woman?
A: She's my mother. She's a teacher.
B: Who is that boy?
A: He's my cousin. He's a student.
B: Your family is nice!

## Unit 7: My toys

### 一、核心词汇

#### 玩具
- toy car 玩具车
- doll 洋娃娃
- ball 球
- kite 风筝
- teddy bear 泰迪熊
- robot 机器人
- puzzle 拼图
- yo-yo 溜溜球

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述玩具
- I have a... 我有一个……
  例：I have a toy car. 我有一个玩具车。
- It's... 它是……
  例：It's red and cool. 它是红色的，很酷。

#### 2. 表达喜好
- I like my... 我喜欢我的……
- It's my favourite toy. 它是我最喜欢的玩具。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的玩具：
1. I have a robot. A. 🤖 B. 🧸
2. My favourite toy is a kite. A. 🪁 B. ⚽

#### 口语练习
描述你最喜欢的玩具：
- My favourite toy is a teddy bear.
- It's brown and soft.
- I like it very much.
- I sleep with it every night.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：toy car, doll, ball, kite, teddy bear, robot, puzzle

### 四、对话

#### 展示玩具
A: Look! I have a new toy.
B: Wow! What is it?
A: It's a robot.
B: Cool! What colour is it?
A: It's blue and white.
B: Can I play with it?
A: Sure! Here you are.
B: Thank you! It's really cool!

## Unit 8: My school

### 一、核心词汇

#### 学校设施
- school 学校
- playground 操场
- library 图书馆
- computer room 计算机房
- music room 音乐室
- art room 美术室
- gym 体育馆
- canteen 食堂

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问位置
- Where is the...? ……在哪里？
- It's on the first/second/third floor. 它在一/二/三层。

#### 2. 描述学校
- This is my school. 这是我的学校。
- It's big/beautiful/new. 它很大/漂亮/新。

#### 3. 表达喜好
- I like my school. 我喜欢我的学校。
- My favourite place is... 我最喜欢的地方是……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的位置：
1. The library is on the first floor. A. 1F B. 2F
2. The music room is on the second floor. A. 1F B. 2F

#### 口语练习
描述你的学校：
- This is my school.
- It's big and beautiful.
- The library is on the first floor.
- The music room is on the second floor.
- My favourite place is the playground.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：school, playground, library, computer room, music room, art room

### 四、对话

#### 参观学校
A: Welcome to our school!
B: Thank you! It's so beautiful!
A: This is the library. It's on the first floor.
B: Wow! So many books!
A: This is the music room. It's on the second floor.
B: Can we sing here?
A: Sure! We have music classes here.
B: Where is the playground?
A: It's behind the teaching building. Let's go and play!
