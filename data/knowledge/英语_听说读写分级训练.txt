# 小学英语听说读写分级训练

## 第一篇：听力训练（按年级分层）

### 低年级（1-2年级）

#### 1. 听音选图
**练习1**：听录音，选择正确的图片。
录音：I have a cat.
选项：A. 🐱  B. 🐶  C. 🐦
答案：A

**练习2**：听录音，选择正确的颜色。
录音：The apple is red.
选项：A. 红色  B. 黄色  C. 绿色
答案：A

#### 2. 听指令做动作
**练习**：听指令，做动作。
- Stand up.（起立）
- Sit down.（坐下）
- Touch your nose.（摸鼻子）
- Clap your hands.（拍手）
- Jump.（跳）

#### 3. 听对话回答问题
**练习**：听对话，回答问题。
录音：
A: How old are you?
B: I'm seven years old.
问题：How old is B?
答案：Seven years old.

### 中年级（3-4年级）

#### 1. 听对话选择答案
**练习**：听对话，选择正确答案。
录音：
A: What's the weather like today?
B: It's sunny and warm.
A: Let's go to the park!
问题：What's the weather like?
答案：It's sunny and warm.

#### 2. 听短文判断正误
**练习**：听短文，判断对错（T/F）。
录音：
My name is Tom. I'm ten years old. I'm in Class 2, Grade 4. I like playing football. I often play football with my friends after school.
1. Tom is nine years old. (F)
2. Tom is in Class 2. (T)
3. Tom likes playing basketball. (F)
4. Tom often plays football after school. (T)

#### 3. 听短文填空
**练习**：听短文，填入所缺单词。
录音：
Today is Monday. The weather is ____. I go to school by ____. I have ____ classes today.
答案：sunny, bus, four

### 高年级（5-6年级）

#### 1. 听长对话回答问题
**练习**：听长对话，回答问题。
录音：
A: What did you do last weekend?
B: I went to the zoo with my family.
A: What animals did you see?
B: I saw monkeys, elephants and pandas.
A: Did you take any photos?
B: Yes, I did. I took many photos.
问题：
1. Where did B go last weekend?
2. What animals did B see?
3. Did B take photos?
答案：
1. B went to the zoo.
2. B saw monkeys, elephants and pandas.
3. Yes, B did.

#### 2. 听短文完成表格
**练习**：听短文，完成表格。
录音：
My name is Li Ming. I'm eleven years old. I'm in Class 3, Grade 5. I like reading books and playing basketball. My favourite subject is English. I want to be a teacher in the future.
表格：
| Name | Age | Class | Hobby | Favourite subject | Dream job |
|------|-----|-------|-------|-------------------|-----------|
|      |     |       |       |                   |           |
答案：
| Name | Age | Class | Hobby | Favourite subject | Dream job |
|------|-----|-------|-------|-------------------|-----------|
| Li Ming | 11 | Class 3, Grade 5 | reading books, playing basketball | English | teacher |

#### 3. 听短文回答问题
**练习**：听短文，回答问题。
录音：
Last summer, I went to Beijing with my parents. We stayed there for five days. On the first day, we visited the Great Wall. It was very long and old. We climbed for three hours. I was very tired, but I was happy. On the second day, we went to the Palace Museum. There were many beautiful things there. We took many photos. On the third day, we visited the Bird's Nest. It was very big and modern. On the fourth day, we went shopping. We bought many gifts. On the last day, we went home. I had a wonderful time in Beijing. I hope I can go there again.
问题：
1. When did the writer go to Beijing?
2. How long did they stay in Beijing?
3. What did they do on the first day?
4. How did the writer feel after climbing the Great Wall?
5. What did they do on the last day?
答案：
1. Last summer.
2. For five days.
3. They visited the Great Wall.
4. Tired but happy.
5. They went home.

## 第二篇：口语训练（按年级分层）

### 低年级（1-2年级）

#### 1. 自我介绍
**模板**：
Hello! My name is ____. I'm ____ years old. I'm in Class ____, Grade ____. I like ____. Nice to meet you!

**练习**：
Hello! My name is Li Ming. I'm seven years old. I'm in Class 1, Grade 2. I like playing football. Nice to meet you!

#### 2. 简单对话
**对话1**：问候
A: Good morning!
B: Good morning! How are you?
A: I'm fine, thank you. And you?
B: I'm great, thanks!

**对话2**：询问物品
A: What's this?
B: It's a pencil.
A: What colour is it?
B: It's blue.

#### 3. 看图说话
**图片**：一个小女孩在公园里放风筝
**描述**：
Look at this picture. It's a sunny day. A girl is in the park. She is flying a kite. The kite is very beautiful. She is very happy.

### 中年级（3-4年级）

#### 1. 描述人物
**模板**：
My friend is ____. He/She is ____ years old. He/She is tall/short. He/She has ____. He/She likes ____.

**练习**：
My friend is Tom. He is ten years old. He is tall and thin. He has short black hair and big eyes. He likes playing basketball.

#### 2. 描述日常活动
**模板**：
I get up at ____. I have breakfast at ____. I go to school at ____. I have ____ classes in the morning. I have lunch at ____. I go home at ____. I do my homework at ____. I go to bed at ____.

**练习**：
I get up at 7 o'clock. I have breakfast at 7:30. I go to school at 8 o'clock. I have four classes in the morning. I have lunch at 12 o'clock. I go home at 4 o'clock. I do my homework at 5 o'clock. I go to bed at 9 o'clock.

#### 3. 角色扮演
**情景**：在餐厅点餐
A: Good evening! Can I help you?
B: Yes, I'd like a hamburger and some French fries.
A: What would you like to drink?
B: A glass of orange juice, please.
A: OK. Here you are.
B: Thank you. How much is it?
A: It's 35 yuan.
B: Here you are.
A: Thank you!

### 高年级（5-6年级）

#### 1. 话题讨论
**话题**：My favourite season
**要点**：
- Which season do you like best?
- Why do you like it?
- What can you do in this season?
- What's the weather like?

**示例**：
I like spring best. Because the weather is warm and the flowers are beautiful. I can go on a picnic with my family. We can fly kites and play in the park. The trees are green and the birds are singing. Everything looks so beautiful. I love spring!

#### 2. 演讲
**话题**：My dream job
**要点**：
- What do you want to be in the future?
- Why do you want to be that?
- What do you need to do to achieve your dream?

**示例**：
Hello, everyone! Today I want to talk about my dream job. I want to be a doctor in the future. Because I want to help sick people and make them healthy. Doctors can save people's lives. That's a great job. To achieve my dream, I need to study hard, especially science and biology. I also need to be kind and patient. I believe I can become a good doctor. Thank you!

#### 3. 辩论
**话题**：Should students use mobile phones at school?
**正方观点**：
- Mobile phones can help students learn.
- Students can use phones to search for information.
- Students can call their parents in emergency.

**反方观点**：
- Mobile phones can distract students from studying.
- Students may play games on phones instead of studying.
- Mobile phones can cause health problems.

## 第三篇：阅读训练（按年级分层）

Look at this picture. It's a sunny day. There are many people in the park. Some people are walking. Some people are sitting on the bench. There are some children playing on the playground. They are very happy. The trees are green and the flowers are beautiful. I like this park very much.

**问题**：
1. What's the weather like?
2. Where are the people?
3. What are the children doing?
4. Are the trees green?

**答案**：
1. It's sunny.
2. They are in the park.
3. They are playing on the playground.
4. Yes, they are.

#### 阅读2

My sister is five years old. She is a student. I am ten years old. I am a student, too. I love my family very much.

**问题**：

2. What does the father do?
3. What does the mother teach?
4. How old is the sister?

**答案**：
1. There are four people.
2. He is a doctor.
3. She teaches English.
4. She is five years old.

## 第四篇：写作训练（按年级分层）

### 低年级（1-2年级）

#### 写作1：My Friend
My friend is Tom. He is seven years old. He is tall and thin. He has short black hair and big eyes. He likes playing football. We often play football together after school. He is very friendly. I like playing with him.

**写作要点**：
- 写清朋友的基本信息
- 描述外貌特征
- 写出共同爱好
- 表达友情

#### 写作2：My Favourite Animal
My favourite animal is the cat. Cats are cute and soft. They have beautiful fur. They like to eat fish. They can catch mice. I have a cat at home. Its name is Mimi. It is white and fluffy. I like playing with Mimi. It is my good friend.

**写作要点**：
- 说明最喜欢的动物
- 描述动物特征
- 写出动物的习性
- 表达喜爱之情

### 中年级（3-4年级）

#### 写作1：My Weekend
Last weekend, I had a wonderful time. On Saturday morning, I got up early and did my homework. In the afternoon, I went to the library with my mother. I borrowed three books. In the evening, I watched TV with my family. On Sunday, we went to the park. The weather was sunny and warm. We had a picnic there. I played with my friends. We flew kites and played games. In the afternoon, we went home. I was tired but happy. It was a great weekend.

**写作要点**：
- 按时间顺序写
- 写清具体活动
- 描述天气和环境
- 表达心情感受

#### 写作2：My Favourite Season
My favourite season is spring. Spring is warm and beautiful. The weather is nice. The trees are green and the flowers are colourful. I can go on a picnic with my family. We can fly kites and play in the park. The birds are singing and the butterflies are dancing. Everything looks so beautiful. I love spring very much!

**写作要点**：
- 说明最喜欢的季节
- 描述季节特点
- 写出可以做的活动
- 表达喜爱之情

### 高年级（5-6年级）

#### 写作1：My Dream Job
Hello, everyone! Today I want to talk about my dream job. I want to be a doctor in the future. There are several reasons. First, I want to help sick people and make them healthy. Doctors can save people's lives. That's a great job. Second, I am interested in science and biology. I like learning about the human body. Third, I am kind and patient. I think these are important qualities for a doctor. To achieve my dream, I need to study hard, especially science and biology. I also need to be responsible and hard-working. I believe I can become a good doctor. Thank you!

**写作要点**：
- 开头点明主题
- 说明reasons
- 阐述需要的努力
- 结尾表达信心

#### 写作2：A Memorable Experience
Last summer, I had a memorable experience. I went to Beijing with my parents. It was my first time to visit the capital of China. We stayed there for five days. On the first day, we visited the Great Wall. It was very long and old. We climbed for three hours. I was very tired, but I was happy. I felt proud to be Chinese. On the second day, we went to the Palace Museum. There were many beautiful things there. I learned a lot about Chinese history. On the third day, we visited the Bird's Nest. It was very big and modern. On the fourth day, we went shopping. We bought many gifts for our friends. On the last day, we went home. I had a wonderful time in Beijing. This experience is very memorable for me. I hope I can go there again.

**写作要点**：
- 开头点明事件
- 按时间顺序写
- 详细描述经历
- 结尾表达感受

## 学习建议

### 1. 听力训练
- 每天听英语音频15分钟
- 选择适合自己水平的材料
- 先听大意，再听细节
- 反复听，直到听懂

### 2. 口语训练
- 每天说英语15分钟
- 模仿标准发音
- 多进行情景对话练习
- 不怕犯错，大胆开口

### 3. 阅读训练
- 每天阅读英语短文15分钟
- 选择适合自己水平的读物
- 先读大意，再读细节
- 积累生词和短语

### 4. 写作训练
- 每周写1-2篇短文
- 从简单到复杂
- 多读范文，学习写作技巧
- 请老师或同学帮忙修改

听说读写是英语学习的四项基本技能，缺一不可。通过系统的训练，可以全面提高英语能力。坚持每天练习，日积月累，英语水平一定会有显著提高！

Remember: Practice makes perfect! 熟能生巧！
