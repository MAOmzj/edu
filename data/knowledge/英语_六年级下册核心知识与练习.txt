# 英语六年级下册核心知识与练习

## Unit 1: How tall are you?

### 核心词汇
- taller 更高的
- shorter 更矮的
- longer 更长的
- older 更老的
- younger 更年轻的
- bigger 更大的
- smaller 更小的
- thinner 更瘦的
- heavier 更重的
- bigger 更大的
- centimetre 厘米
- kilogram 千克

### 核心句型
- How tall are you? 你多高？
- I'm... metres tall. 我……米高。
- How heavy are you? 你多重？
- I'm... kilograms. 我……千克。
- You're older/taller/heavier than me. 你比我更老/更高/更重。
- How old are you? 你多大了？
- I'm... years old. 我……岁了。

### 语法重点
形容词比较级：
- 单音节词加-er：tall→taller, short→shorter, old→older
- 以e结尾加-r：large→larger, nice→nicer
- 辅音字母+y结尾，变y为i加-er：heavy→heavier, happy→happier
- 多音节词前加more：beautiful→more beautiful

### 重点练习
比较两个人或事物的特征。

### 情景对话示例
A: How tall are you?
B: I'm 1.6 metres tall.
A: How heavy are you?
B: I'm 48 kilograms.
A: You're taller than me. I'm 1.55 metres tall.

## Unit 2: Last weekend

### 核心词汇
- cleaned 打扫
- stayed 停留
- washed 洗
- watched 观看
- stayed 停留
- had 有
- slept 睡觉
- read 读
- saw 看见
- last weekend 上个周末
- yesterday 昨天
- the day before yesterday 前天
- last night 昨晚
- last month 上个月
- last year 去年

### 核心句型
- What did you do last weekend? 你上周末做了什么？
- I watched TV/washed my clothes/cleaned my room. 我看电视/洗衣服/打扫房间了。
- Did you...? 你……了吗？
- Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

### 语法重点
一般过去时：
- 规则动词过去式：加-ed（played, watched, cleaned）
- 以e结尾加-d（liked, lived）
- 辅音字母+y结尾，变y为i加-ed（studied, carried）
- 重读闭音节双写末尾辅音字母加-ed（stopped, planned）
- 不规则动词过去式：see→saw, read→read, have→had, go→went

### 重点练习
描述过去发生的事情。

### 情景对话示例
A: What did you do last weekend?
B: I watched TV and cleaned my room.
A: Did you do anything else?
B: Yes, I played football with my friends.
A: That sounds great!

## Unit 3: Where did you go?

### 核心词汇
- went 去
- camped 野营
- took 拍照
- bought 买
- gift 礼物
- ate 吃
- photo 照片
- fell 摔倒
- hurt 受伤
- rode 骑
- could 能
- till 直到
- beach 海滩

### 核心句型
- Where did you go? 你去哪里了？
- I went to... 我去了……
- What did you do there? 你在那里做了什么？
- I rode a bike/took photos/bought gifts. 我骑自行车/拍照/买礼物了。
- How was your weekend/holiday? 你的周末/假期怎么样？
- It was good/bad. 很好/不好。

### 语法重点
一般过去时的综合运用：
- 询问过去的事情：Where did you go? What did you do?
- 描述过去的经历：I went to... I took photos.

### 重点练习
描述过去的旅行或假期经历。

### 情景对话示例
A: Where did you go last holiday?
B: I went to Sanya.
A: What did you do there?
B: I swam in the sea and took many photos.
A: How was your holiday?
B: It was great!

## Unit 4: Then and now

### 核心词汇
- dining hall 餐厅
- grass 草坪
- gym 体育馆
- ago 以前
- cycling 骑自行车
- ice-skate 滑冰
- badminton 羽毛球
- star 星星
- easy 容易的
- look up 查阅
- different 不同的
- active 活跃的

### 核心句型
- There was/were... 以前有……
- There is/are... 现在有……
- I didn't... before. Now I... 以前我不……。现在我……。
- Before, I was... Now I'm... 以前我是……。现在我是……。
- Did you use to...? 你过去常常……吗？
- Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

### 语法重点
一般过去时与一般现在时的对比：
- 描述过去的情况：There was a library before. I didn't like sports.
- 描述现在的情况：There is a new library now. I like sports now.

### 重点练习
描述过去的变化和现在的不同。

### 情景对话示例
A: What was your school like five years ago?
B: There was no library. There was no gym.
A: What about now?
B: Now there's a new library and a big gym.
A: Did you use to like sports?
B: No, I didn't. But now I like badminton.

## 总复习

### 一、语法总结

#### 1. 时态
- 一般现在时：描述经常性、习惯性的动作或状态
  - I go to school every day.
  - He goes to school every day.
- 一般过去时：描述过去发生的动作或状态
  - I went to the park yesterday.
  - She watched TV last night.
- 一般将来时：描述将要发生的动作或状态
  - I'm going to visit my grandma tomorrow.
  - We will have a party next week.
- 现在进行时：描述正在进行的动作
  - I'm reading a book now.
  - They are playing football.

#### 2. 形容词比较级
- 单音节：tall→taller, short→shorter
- 以e结尾：large→larger, nice→nicer
- 辅音+y：heavy→heavier, happy→happier
- 多音节：beautiful→more beautiful

#### 3. 特殊疑问词
- What：什么
- Where：哪里
- When：什么时候
- Who：谁
- How：怎样
- Why：为什么
- Which：哪一个

### 二、话题总结

#### 1. 比较
- How tall/heavy/old are you?
- You're taller/heavier/older than me.

#### 2. 过去的事情
- What did you do last weekend?
- Where did you go last holiday?
- I watched TV/played football.

#### 3. 变化
- There was/were... before. Now there is/are...
- I didn't... before. Now I...

#### 4. 将来计划
- What are you going to do this weekend?
- I'm going to...

### 三、重点词汇分类

#### 1. 比较级词汇
taller, shorter, longer, older, younger, bigger, smaller, thinner, heavier

#### 2. 过去式词汇
规则动词：cleaned, stayed, washed, watched, played
不规则动词：went, took, bought, ate, saw, read, had, rode

#### 3. 时间词汇
yesterday, last weekend, last night, last month, last year, ago

#### 4. 地点词汇
park, zoo, cinema, library, gym, beach, camp

### 四、重点句型

1. How tall/heavy/old are you?
2. You're...than me.
3. What did you do last weekend?
4. Where did you go last holiday?
5. I went to... I...
6. There was/were... before. Now there is/are...
7. I didn't... before. Now I...
8. What are you going to do?
9. I'm going to...

### 五、情景对话

1. 比较身高、体重、年龄
2. 谈论上周末的活动
3. 描述过去的假期
4. 谈论学校的变化
5. 讨论未来的计划

## 小升初英语复习建议

### 1. 系统梳理语法
- 熟练掌握四种时态
- 理解形容词比较级的构成和用法
- 掌握特殊疑问词的用法

### 2. 积累核心词汇
- 分类记忆单词
- 注意动词的过去式
- 熟记常用短语

### 3. 强化听说能力
- 每天听英语音频15分钟
- 模仿标准发音
- 进行情景对话练习

### 4. 提高读写能力
- 每天阅读英语短文
- 练习写作（日记、邮件）
- 积累好词好句

### 5. 做综合练习
- 完成小升初模拟题
- 分析错题，查漏补缺
- 提高应试能力

## 学习建议
- 熟练掌握形容词比较级
- 牢记不规则动词的过去式
- 区分不同时态的用法
- 多进行情景对话练习
- 为小升初做好充分准备
