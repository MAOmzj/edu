# 英语四年级下册同步练习

## Unit 1: My school

### 一、核心词汇

#### 学校设施
- first floor 一楼
- second floor 二楼
- teacher's office 教师办公室
- library 图书馆
- playground 操场
- computer room 计算机房
- art room 美术室
- music room 音乐室

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问位置
- Where is the...? ……在哪里？
- It's on the first/second floor. 它在一/二楼。

#### 2. 描述位置
- Is this the...? 这是……吗？
- Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

#### 3. 提出建议
- Let's go to the... 让我们去……吧！
- OK! / Let's go!

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的位置：
1. The library is on the first floor. A. 1F B. 2F
2. The art room is on the second floor. A. 1F B. 2F

#### 口语练习
描述你的学校：
- This is my school.
- The library is on the first floor.
- The music room is on the second floor.
- Let's go to the playground!

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：first floor, second floor, teacher's office, library, playground

### 四、对话

#### 参观学校
A: Welcome to our school!
B: Thank you! Where is the library?
A: It's on the first floor. This way, please.
B: Wow! So many books!
A: Yes! Let's go to the art room. It's on the second floor.
B: OK! Let's go!

## Unit 2: What time is it?

### 一、核心词汇

#### 时间
- o'clock ……点钟
- thirty 三十
- forty 四十
- fifty 五十

#### 时间表达
- It's time for... 到……的时间了
- It's time to... 到做……的时间了

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问时间
- What time is it? 现在几点了？
- It's... o'clock. ……点钟。

#### 2. 表达该做什么
- It's time for English class. 到英语课的时间了。
- It's time to go to school. 到上学的时间了。

#### 3. 催促
- Hurry up! 快点！
- It's time to... 到……的时间了。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的时间：
1. It's 7 o'clock. A. 7:00 B. 8:00
2. It's time for lunch. A. 11:00 B. 12:00

#### 口语练习
描述你的一天：
- It's 7 o'clock. It's time to get up.
- It's 8 o'clock. It's time to go to school.
- It's 12 o'clock. It's time for lunch.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：o'clock, thirty, forty, fifty

### 四、对话

#### 日常作息
A: What time is it?
B: It's 7 o'clock.
A: It's time to get up!
B: OK! What time is it now?
A: It's 8 o'clock. It's time to go to school.
B: Hurry up!

## Unit 3: Weather

### 一、核心词汇

#### 天气
- warm 温暖的
- hot 热的
- cool 凉爽的
- cold 冷的
- sunny 晴朗的
- windy 有风的
- cloudy 多云的
- rainy 下雨的
- snowy 下雪的

#### 地点
- Beijing 北京
- New York 纽约
- Toronto 多伦多
- Sydney 悉尼

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问天气
- What's the weather like in...? ……的天气怎么样？
- It's... 天气是……

#### 2. 表达能否做某事
- Can I go outside now? 我现在能出去吗？
- Yes, you can. / No, you can't.

#### 3. 建议
- It's warm today. Let's play football. 今天很暖和，让我们踢足球吧。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的天气：
1. It's sunny in Beijing. A. ☀️ B. ☁️
2. It's rainy in New York. A. 🌧️ B. ⛄

#### 口语练习
报告天气：
- Good morning! Today is Monday.
- It's sunny and warm in Beijing.
- It's rainy in New York.
- It's snowy in Toronto.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：warm, hot, cool, cold, sunny, windy, cloudy, rainy, snowy

### 四、对话

#### 谈论天气
A: What's the weather like in Beijing?
B: It's warm and sunny.
A: Can I wear my T-shirt?
B: Yes, you can.
A: What about New York?
B: It's rainy. You can't go outside.

## Unit 4: At the farm

### 一、核心词汇

#### 蔬菜
- tomato 西红柿
- potato 土豆
- carrot 胡萝卜
- onion 洋葱

#### 动物
- cow 奶牛
- horse 马
- sheep 绵羊
- hen 母鸡
- goat 山羊

#### 数量
- these 这些
- those 那些

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问物品
- What are these/those? 这些/那些是什么？
- They're... 它们是……

#### 2. 询问数量
- How many...? 有多少……？
- There are... 有……

#### 3. 表达喜好
- I like... 我喜欢……
- Do you like...? 你喜欢……吗？
- Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的动物：
1. These are cows. A. 🐄 B. 🐴
2. Those are sheep. A. 🐑 B. 🐐

#### 口语练习
描述农场：
- These are tomatoes. They're red.
- Those are cows. They're black and white.
- How many horses? There are five.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：tomato, potato, carrot, cow, horse, sheep, hen

### 四、对话

#### 在农场
A: Look! What are these?
B: They're tomatoes. They're red and big.
A: What are those?
B: They're cows. They're black and white.
A: How many horses?
B: There are five horses.
A: Do you like goats?
B: Yes, I do. They're cute!

## Unit 5: My clothes

### 一、核心词汇

#### 衣服
- pants 裤子
- dress 连衣裙
- skirt 裙子
- coat 外套
- sweater 毛衣
- jacket 夹克
- shirt 衬衫
- shorts 短裤
- socks 袜子
- shoes 鞋子

#### 物品归属
- yours 你的
- mine 我的
- his 他的
- hers 她的

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问归属
- Whose...is this? 这是谁的……？
- It's mine/yours/his/hers. 是我的/你的/他的/她的。

#### 2. 询问颜色
- What colour is it? 它是什么颜色？
- It's... 它是……色的。

#### 3. 描述穿着
- I'm wearing... 我穿着……
- You're wearing... 你穿着……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的衣服：
1. These are my pants. A. 👖 B. 👗
2. That dress is hers. A. 👗 B. 👔

#### 口语练习
描述你的衣服：
- These are my pants. They're blue.
- This is my dress. It's red.
- That coat is mine. It's warm.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：pants, dress, skirt, coat, sweater, jacket, shirt

### 四、对话

#### 整理衣物
A: Whose pants are these?
B: They're mine.
A: Whose dress is that?
B: It's my sister's.
A: What colour is your coat?
B: It's black. It's very warm.

## Unit 6: Shopping

### 一、核心词汇

#### 购物
- expensive 昂贵的
- cheap 便宜的
- nice 好看的
- pretty 漂亮的
- colourful 多彩的

#### 尺码
- size 尺码
- small 小的
- medium 中等的
- large 大的
- try 试穿

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问价格
- How much is/are...? ……多少钱？
- It's... yuan. ……元。

#### 2. 评价物品
- It's expensive/cheap. 它很贵/便宜。
- It's nice/pretty/colourful. 它很好看/漂亮/多彩。

#### 3. 购物对话
- Can I help you? 我能帮你吗？
- I'd like... 我想要……
- How much is it? 多少钱？
- It's... yuan. ……元。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的价格：
1. The shirt is 80 yuan. A. 💰80 B. 💰90
2. The dress is cheap. It's 50 yuan. A. 💰50 B. 💰150

#### 口语练习
模拟购物：
- Can I help you?
- I'd like a T-shirt, please.
- What colour?
- Blue, please.
- How much is it?
- It's 45 yuan.
- OK, I'll take it.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：expensive, cheap, nice, pretty, colourful, size

### 四、对话

#### 在商店
A: Can I help you?
B: Yes, I'd like a dress, please.
A: What colour do you like?
B: I like pink.
A: How about this one?
B: It's pretty! Can I try it on?
A: Sure. The fitting room is over there.
B: It fits well. How much is it?
A: It's 120 yuan.
B: OK, I'll take it.

## Unit 7: My school things

### 一、核心词汇

#### 学习用品
- pencil 铅笔
- ruler 尺子
- eraser 橡皮
- sharpener 卷笔刀
- pen 钢笔
- book 书
- notebook 笔记本
- bag 书包

#### 位置
- on 在……上面
- in 在……里面
- under 在……下面
- near 在……附近

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问位置
- Where is my...? 我的……在哪里？
- It's in/on/under/near... 它在……里面/上面/下面/附近。

#### 2. 描述拥有
- I have... 我有……
- I don't have... 我没有……

#### 3. 询问数量
- How many...? 有多少……？
- I have... 我有……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的位置：
1. The pencil is in the pencil box. A. ✏️ B. 📦
2. The book is on the desk. A. 📚 B. 📖

#### 口语练习
描述你的学习用品：
- I have a pencil box.
- There is a pencil, a ruler and an eraser in it.
- My book is on the desk.
- I have 5 notebooks.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：pencil, ruler, eraser, sharpener, pen, book, notebook

### 四、对话

#### 找东西
A: Where is my pencil?
B: Is it in your pencil box?
A: No, it isn't.
B: Is it on the desk?
A: No, it isn't.
B: Is it under the chair?
A: Yes, it is! Thank you!
B: You're welcome!

## Unit 8: My day

### 一、核心词汇

#### 日常活动
- get up 起床
- go to school 上学
- have breakfast 吃早餐
- have lunch 吃午餐
- have dinner 吃晚餐
- go to bed 上床睡觉
- watch TV 看电视
- read books 读书
- do homework 做作业

#### 时间
- morning 早上
- afternoon 下午
- evening 晚上
- night 晚上

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述日常
- I...in the morning. 我早上……
  例：I get up at 7 o'clock in the morning. 我早上7点起床。

#### 2. 询问时间
- What time do you...? 你什么时候……？
- I...at... 我在……点……

#### 3. 表达频率
- I always/often/sometimes... 我总是/经常/有时……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的时间：
1. I get up at 7 o'clock. A. 7:00 B. 8:00
2. I go to bed at 9 o'clock. A. 9:00 B. 10:00

#### 口语练习
描述你的一天：
- I get up at 7 o'clock in the morning.
- I have breakfast at 7:30.
- I go to school at 8 o'clock.
- I have lunch at 12 o'clock.
- I go home at 4 o'clock in the afternoon.
- I do my homework at 5 o'clock.
- I have dinner at 6 o'clock in the evening.
- I go to bed at 9 o'clock at night.

#### 读写练习
抄写词组：get up, go to school, have breakfast, have lunch, have dinner, go to bed

### 四、对话

#### 谈论日常
A: What time do you get up?
B: I get up at 7 o'clock.
A: What do you do in the morning?
B: I have breakfast and go to school.
A: What do you do after school?
B: I do my homework and watch TV.
A: What time do you go to bed?
B: I go to bed at 9 o'clock.
