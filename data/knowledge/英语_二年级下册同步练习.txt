# 英语二年级下册同步练习

## Unit 1: My Classroom

### 一、核心词汇

#### 教室物品
- desk 书桌
- chair 椅子
- blackboard 黑板
- door 门
- window 窗户
- light 灯
- clock 钟
- map 地图

#### 位置介词
- in 在……里面
- on 在……上面
- under 在……下面
- near 在……附近
- behind 在……后面
- in front of 在……前面
- next to 在……旁边

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 询问位置
- Where is the...? ……在哪里？
- It's in/on/under... 它在……里面/上面/下面

#### 2. 描述位置
- There is/are... 有……
  例：There is a clock on the wall. 墙上有一个钟。

#### 3. 指令
- Open the door/window. 开门/窗。
- Close the door/window. 关门/窗。
- Turn on/off the light. 开/关灯。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听指令做动作：
1. Open the window.
2. Turn on the light.
3. Close the door.

#### 口语练习
描述你的教室：
- There is a blackboard on the wall.
- There are many desks and chairs.
- There is a clock above the blackboard.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：desk, chair, blackboard, door, window, light, clock, map

### 四、对话

#### 在教室
A: Where is my pencil?
B: It's on your desk.
A: Thank you! Where is my book?
B: It's under the chair.

## Unit 2: My Family (Extended)

### 一、核心词汇

#### 家庭成员（扩展）
- uncle 叔叔/舅舅
- aunt 阿姨/姑姑
- cousin 堂/表兄弟姐妹
- son 儿子
- daughter 女儿
- parent 父母

#### 职业
- doctor 医生
- teacher 老师
- nurse 护士
- farmer 农民
- driver 司机
- cook 厨师
- police officer 警察
- singer 歌手
- dancer 舞者
- artist 艺术家

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 介绍家人的职业
- What does your father/mother do? 你爸爸/妈妈是做什么的？
- He's/She's a... 他/她是一个……
  例：He's a doctor. 他是医生。

#### 2. 描述家人的特征
- My father is... 我爸爸是……
  例：My father is tall and strong. 我爸爸又高又壮。
- My mother has... 我妈妈有……
  例：My mother has long hair. 我妈妈有长头发。

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的职业：
1. He's a doctor. A. 👨‍⚕️ B. 👨‍🏫
2. She's a nurse. A. 👩‍⚕️ B. 👩‍🏫

#### 口语练习
介绍你的家人：
- This is my father. He's tall. He's a doctor.
- This is my mother. She's beautiful. She's a teacher.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：uncle, aunt, cousin, doctor, teacher, nurse, farmer

### 四、对话

#### 介绍家人
A: Who is that man?
B: He's my uncle.
A: What does he do?
B: He's a driver.
A: Who is that woman?
B: She's my aunt. She's a nurse.

## Unit 3: Food and Drinks (Extended)

### 一、核心词汇

#### 食物（扩展）
- sandwich 三明治
- hamburger 汉堡包
- pizza 比萨饼
- noodles 面条
- rice 米饭
- chicken 鸡肉
- beef 牛肉
- fish 鱼
- egg 鸡蛋
- vegetable 蔬菜
- fruit 水果
- soup 汤

#### 饮料（扩展）
- juice 果汁
- milk 牛奶
- water 水
- tea 茶
- coffee 咖啡
- cola 可乐
- soda 苏打水

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 点餐
- What would you like? 你想要什么？
- I'd like some..., please. 我想要一些……
- Here you are. 给你。
- Thank you. 谢谢。

#### 2. 询问价格
- How much is it? 多少钱？
- It's... yuan. ……元。

#### 3. 表达喜好
- I like... 我喜欢……
- I don't like... 我不喜欢……
- My favorite food is... 我最喜欢的食物是……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的食物：
1. I'd like some noodles. A. 🍜 B. 🍝
2. How much is the pizza? It's 30 yuan. A. 🍕 B. 🍔

#### 口语练习
模拟点餐：
A: What would you like?
B: I'd like a hamburger and some juice.
A: Here you are.
B: Thank you. How much is it?
A: It's 25 yuan.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：sandwich, hamburger, pizza, noodles, rice, chicken, beef, fish

### 四、对话

#### 在餐厅
A: Welcome to our restaurant! Can I help you?
B: Yes, I'd like a sandwich and some soup.
A: What would you like to drink?
B: Some juice, please.
A: OK. Here you are.
B: Thank you. How much is it?
A: It's 35 yuan.
B: Here you are.
A: Thank you!

## Unit 4: Animals at the Zoo

### 一、核心词汇

#### 动物（扩展）
- lion 狮子
- tiger 老虎
- bear 熊
- elephant 大象
- monkey 猴子
- panda 熊猫
- zebra 斑马
- giraffe 长颈鹿
- kangaroo 袋鼠
- frog 青蛙
- snake 蛇
- bird 鸟
- fish 鱼
- butterfly 蝴蝶
- bee 蜜蜂

#### 动物特征
- big 大的
- small 小的
- tall 高的
- short 矮的
- long 长的
- cute 可爱的
- scary 可怕的
- friendly 友好的

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述动物
- The... is... ……是……
  例：The elephant is big. 大象很大。
- The... has... ……有……
  例：The giraffe has a long neck. 长颈鹿有长脖子。

#### 2. 询问能力
- Can it...? 它会……吗？
- Yes, it can. / No, it can't.

#### 3. 表达喜好
- I like... 我喜欢……
- My favorite animal is... 我最喜欢的动物是……
- I like... because... 我喜欢……因为……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听描述，猜动物：
1. It's big and has a long nose. It's an elephant.
2. It's small and can jump. It's a frog.

#### 口语练习
描述你喜欢的动物：
- My favorite animal is the panda.
- It's black and white.
- It's cute and fat.
- I like pandas because they're friendly.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：lion, tiger, bear, elephant, monkey, panda, zebra, giraffe

### 四、对话

#### 在动物园
A: Look! What's that?
B: It's a lion.
A: Wow! It's big and scary!
B: Don't be afraid. Let's go see the pandas.
A: Great! I love pandas!
B: Here they are. They're eating bamboo.
A: They're so cute!

## Unit 5: Clothes and Weather

### 一、核心词汇

#### 衣服（扩展）
- T-shirt T恤衫
- shirt 衬衫
- dress 连衣裙
- skirt 裙子
- pants/trousers 裤子
- shorts 短裤
- jeans 牛仔裤
- shoes 鞋子
- socks 袜子
- hat 帽子
- coat 外套
- jacket 夹克
- sweater 毛衣
- boots 靴子
- scarf 围巾
- gloves 手套

#### 天气
- sunny 晴朗的
- cloudy 多云的
- rainy 下雨的
- snowy 下雪的
- windy 有风的
- hot 热的
- cold 冷的
- warm 温暖的
- cool 凉爽的

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述穿着
- I'm wearing... 我穿着……
  例：I'm wearing a T-shirt and shorts. 我穿着T恤和短裤。

#### 2. 根据天气选择衣服
- It's cold. Put on your coat. 天冷了，穿上外套。
- It's hot. Take off your coat. 天热了，脱掉外套。

#### 3. 询问天气
- What's the weather like today? 今天天气怎么样？
- It's... 天气是……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的衣服：
1. It's cold. Put on your sweater. A. 🧥 B. 👕
2. It's hot. Take off your coat. A. 🧥 B. 👕

#### 口语练习
根据天气描述穿着：
- Today is sunny and hot.
- I'm wearing a T-shirt and shorts.
- I'm wearing sunglasses.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：T-shirt, dress, skirt, pants, shoes, socks, hat, coat, jacket, sweater

### 四、对话

#### 谈论天气和穿着
A: What's the weather like today?
B: It's cold and windy.
A: What are you wearing?
B: I'm wearing a sweater and jeans.
A: Me too! Let's go to the park.
B: OK! But put on your jacket. It's windy.

## Unit 6: My Day (Extended)

### 一、核心词汇

#### 日常活动（扩展）
- get up 起床
- brush teeth 刷牙
- wash face 洗脸
- have breakfast 吃早餐
- go to school 上学
- have classes 上课
- have lunch 吃午餐
- go home 回家
- do homework 做作业
- have dinner 吃晚餐
- watch TV 看电视
- read books 读书
- play games 玩游戏
- take a shower 洗澡
- go to bed 上床睡觉

#### 时间表达
- in the morning 在早上
- in the afternoon 在下午
- in the evening 在晚上
- at night 在晚上
- at 7 o'clock 在7点
- half past 7 7点半
- quarter past 7 7点15分
- quarter to 8 7点45分

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述日常
- I...in the morning. 我早上……
  例：I get up at 7 o'clock in the morning. 我早上7点起床。
- I...in the afternoon. 我下午……
- I...in the evening. 我晚上……

#### 2. 询问时间
- What time do you...? 你什么时候……？
- I...at... 我在……点……
  例：What time do you get up? I get up at 7.

#### 3. 询问频率
- How often do you...? 你多久……一次？
- I...every day/every week. 我每天/每周……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的时间：
1. I get up at 7 o'clock. A. 7:00 B. 8:00
2. I go to bed at 9 o'clock. A. 9:00 B. 10:00

#### 口语练习
描述你的一天：
- I get up at 7 o'clock in the morning.
- I brush my teeth and wash my face.
- I have breakfast at 7:30.
- I go to school at 8 o'clock.
- I have lunch at 12 o'clock.
- I go home at 4 o'clock in the afternoon.
- I do my homework at 5 o'clock.
- I have dinner at 6 o'clock in the evening.
- I watch TV at 7 o'clock.
- I go to bed at 9 o'clock at night.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：get up, brush teeth, wash face, have breakfast, go to school, do homework

### 四、对话

#### 谈论日常
A: What time do you get up?
B: I get up at 7 o'clock.
A: What do you do in the morning?
B: I brush my teeth, wash my face, and have breakfast.
A: What do you do after school?
B: I do my homework and play games.
A: What time do you go to bed?
B: I go to bed at 9 o'clock.

## Unit 7: Holidays and Festivals

### 一、核心词汇

#### 节日（扩展）
- Spring Festival 春节
- Lantern Festival 元宵节
- Qingming Festival 清明节
- Dragon Boat Festival 端午节
- Mid-Autumn Festival 中秋节
- Double Ninth Festival 重阳节
- Christmas 圣诞节
- Halloween 万圣节
- Thanksgiving 感恩节
- Easter 复活节
- Valentine's Day 情人节
- New Year's Day 元旦

#### 节日活动
- eat dumplings 吃饺子
- eat mooncakes 吃月饼
- eat zongzi 吃粽子
- watch dragon boat races 看龙舟赛
- climb mountains 爬山
- give red envelopes 给红包
- give presents 送礼物
- dress up 装扮
- trick or treat 不给糖就捣蛋

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 介绍节日
- ... is a... holiday. ……是一个……节日。
  例：Spring Festival is a Chinese holiday. 春节是一个中国节日。

#### 2. 描述活动
- We...on... 我们在…………
  例：We eat dumplings on Spring Festival. 我们在春节吃饺子。

#### 3. 表达喜好
- I like... 我喜欢……
- My favorite holiday is... 我最喜欢的节日是……
- I like... because... 我喜欢……因为……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的节日：
1. We eat mooncakes. A. 🥮 Mid-Autumn Festival B. 🎄 Christmas
2. We give presents. A. 🎁 Christmas B. 🏮 Spring Festival

#### 口语练习
介绍你喜欢的节日：
- My favorite holiday is Spring Festival.
- We eat dumplings and give red envelopes.
- I like it because I can get lucky money and play with my cousins.

#### 读写练习
抄写单词：Spring Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival, Christmas, Dragon Boat Festival

### 四、对话

#### 谈论节日
A: What's your favorite holiday?
B: I like Spring Festival best.
A: Why?
B: Because we can eat dumplings and get red envelopes.
A: When is Spring Festival?
B: It's usually in January or February.
A: What do you do on that day?
B: We have a big family dinner and watch TV together.

## Unit 8: Seasons and Activities

### 一、核心词汇

#### 季节活动
- spring: fly a kite 放风筝, plant trees 植树, have a picnic 野餐
- summer: swim 游泳, eat ice cream 吃冰淇淋, go to the beach 去海滩
- autumn: pick apples 摘苹果, fly a kite 放风筝, climb mountains 爬山
- winter: make a snowman 堆雪人, ski 滑雪, skate 滑冰

### 二、核心句型

#### 1. 描述季节活动
- In..., I can... 在……，我可以……
  例：In summer, I can swim. 在夏天，我可以游泳。

#### 2. 询问喜欢的季节
- What's your favorite season? 你最喜欢哪个季节？
- My favorite season is... 我最喜欢的季节是……
- I like... because I can... 我喜欢……因为我可以……

#### 3. 询问活动
- What do you do in...? 你在……做什么？
- I... 我……

### 三、练习

#### 听力练习
听录音，选择正确的季节：
1. I can swim in the sea. A. ☀️ summer B. ❄️ winter
2. I can make a snowman. A. 🌸 spring B. ⛄ winter

#### 口语练习
描述你喜欢的季节和活动：
- My favorite season is summer.
- I can swim in the pool.
- I can eat ice cream.
- I can go to the beach with my family.

#### 读写练习
抄写词组：fly a kite, swim, eat ice cream, make a snowman, ski, skate

### 四、歌曲

#### 《Seasons Song》
In spring, I fly a kite.
In summer, I swim in the sea.
In autumn, I pick apples.
In winter, I make a snowman.
I love all the seasons!
