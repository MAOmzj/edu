小学英语语法与阅读写作专题百科

本资料是《英语基础》和两套词汇书的配套专题，系统整理小学阶段核心语法要点、阅读理解技巧和写作范文。语法是语言的规则，阅读是输入的通道，写作是输出的表达——三者结合才能真正用好英语。

============================================================
第一篇：核心语法精讲
============================================================

一、名词复数变化规则总结

【规则变化】
一般情况加 -s：book→books, pen→pens, day→days
以 s, x, ch, sh 结尾加 -es：bus→buses, box→boxes, watch→watches
辅音字母 + y 结尾，变 y 为 i 加 -es：baby→babies, city→cities, story→stories
以 f 或 fe 结尾，变 f/fe 为 v 加 -es：knife→knives, leaf→leaves, life→lives
以 o 结尾有生命的加 -es：tomato→tomatoes, potato→potatoes
以 o 结尾无生命的加 -s：photo→photos, piano→pianos

【不规则变化】
man→men, woman→women, child→children
foot→feet, tooth→teeth, mouse→mice
fish→fish, sheep→sheep, deer→deer, Chinese→Chinese

【单复数同形要牢记】
fish, sheep, deer, Chinese, Japanese 单复数一样。
注意：man 和 woman 修饰复数名词时也要变：two men teachers。

二、冠词 a/an/the 用法总结

【a 和 an 的区别】
看发音不看字母！
a + 辅音音素开头的词：a book, a car, a uniform（u发/juː/辅音）, a useful book
an + 元音音素开头的词：an apple, an egg, an hour（h不发音）, an honest boy

【the 的用法】
特指：Close the door, please.（双方都知道的那扇门）
独一无二：the sun, the moon, the earth
第二次提到：I have a cat. The cat is white.
序数词前：the first, the second
最高级前：the tallest, the best
乐器前：play the piano, play the guitar

【不用 the 的情况】
球类运动：play football/basketball（不加 the）
三餐前：have breakfast/lunch/dinner
by + 交通工具：by bus, by bike, by plane
国名、人名前：China, Tom

三、代词用法总结

【人称代词对照表】
人称 | 主格（作主语） | 宾格（作宾语）
I | I | me
you | you | you
he/she/it | he/she/it | him/her/it
we | we | us
they | they | them

口诀：主格在动词前（I like her），宾格在动词后或介词后（She helps me. Look at him.）

【物主代词对照表】
人称 | 形容词性（+名词） | 名词性（不+名词）
I | my (my book) | mine
you | your (your bag) | yours
he/she/it | his/her/its (his pen) | his/hers/its
we | our (our school) | ours
they | their (their room) | theirs

口诀：形容词性物主代词后面必须跟名词，名词性物主代词后面不能跟名词。
This is my book. = This book is mine.

四、be 动词用法口诀

I 用 am，you 用 are，is 用于 he/she/it。
单数名词用 is，复数名词用 are。
否定句：be 动词后加 not（is not = isn't, are not = aren't, am not 无缩写）
一般疑问句：be 动词提到句首。Are you a student? Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

五、一般现在时三单变化

主语是第三人称单数（he/she/it/单个人名/单个事物）时，动词要加 s/es。
变化规则与名词复数相同：
一般加 -s：play→plays, like→likes, read→reads
以 s, x, ch, sh, o 结尾加 -es：watch→watches, go→goes, do→does
辅音 + y 结尾变 y 为 i 加 -es：study→studies, carry→carries

否定句和疑问句借助 do/does：
I/you/we/they + don't + 动词原形：I don't like milk.
he/she/it + doesn't + 动词原形：He doesn't like milk.
Do + I/you/we/they + 动词原形？：Do you like apples?
Does + he/she/it + 动词原形？：Does he go to school?

⚠️ does 后面动词恢复原形！Does he go?（不说 Does he goes?）

六、现在进行时

结构：主语 + am/is/are + 动词ing
动词 ing 变化规则：
一般直接加 -ing：play→playing, read→reading
以不发音 e 结尾去 e 加 -ing：make→making, write→writing, dance→dancing
重读闭音节末尾辅音双写加 -ing：run→running, swim→swimming, sit→sitting
标志词：now, look!, listen!, at the moment

七、一般过去时

结构：主语 + 动词过去式
规则变化：一般加 -ed，以 e 结尾加 -d，辅音+y 变 i 加 -ed，重读闭音节双写加 -ed。
不规则动词必背（高频）：
go→went, come→came, have→had, do→did, eat→ate, drink→drank
see→saw, read→read, write→wrote, take→took, buy→bought
get→got, run→ran, swim→swam, sing→sang, fly→flew
ride→rode, sit→sat, give→gave, make→made, say→said
tell→told, win→won, meet→met, feel→felt, sleep→slept
keep→kept, leave→left, lose→lost, put→put, cut→cut
is/am→was, are→were

⚠️ 否定句：didn't + 动词原形。I didn't go.（不说 didn't went）
疑问句：Did + 主语 + 动词原形？Did you finish?

八、一般将来时

will 结构：主语 + will + 动词原形
否定：will not = won't
疑问：Will + 主语 + 动词原形？

be going to 结构：主语 + am/is/are going to + 动词原形
表示有计划打算或根据迹象推测。
I am going to visit my grandma tomorrow.（有计划）
Look at the clouds. It's going to rain.（根据迹象推测）

九、情态动词

can：表示能力、许可。后接动词原形，不随主语变化。
He can swim. Can you help me?

must：表示必须。mustn't 表示禁止。
We must follow the rules. You mustn't run in the street.

should：表示应该、建议。后接动词原形。
You should drink more water.

will：表示将要。否定 won't。

⚠️ 情态动词后面都接动词原形，不随主语变化，否定直接在情态动词后加 not。

十、常考句型转换

【陈述句→一般疑问句】
有 be 动词：be 提到句首。He is happy. → Is he happy?
有情态动词：情态动词提到句首。She can sing. → Can she sing?
只有实义动词：加 Do/Does。He likes apples. → Does he like apples?

【陈述句→否定句】
有 be 动词：be 后加 not。She is a teacher. → She isn't a teacher.
有情态动词：情态动词后加 not。I can swim. → I can't swim.
只有实义动词：加 don't/doesn't。He plays football. → He doesn't play football.

【对画线部分提问】
先判断画线部分是什么（人/物/时间/地点/原因/方式），再选对应的疑问词。
what（什么）, who（谁）, whose（谁的）, where（哪里）, when（什么时候）
what time（几点）, why（为什么）, how（怎样）, how many（多少-可数）
how much（多少-不可数/多少钱）, how old（多大）

步骤：疑问词 + 一般疑问句语序。
He is reading a book.（对 a book 提问）→ What is he reading?
She goes to school by bus.（对 by bus 提问）→ How does she go to school?

第二篇：阅读理解技巧
============================================================

十一、阅读步骤

第一步：看标题和图片，猜文章主题。
第二步：快速通读全文，了解大意，不必每个词都读懂。
第三步：读题目，带着问题回文中找答案。
第四步：联系上下文猜生词意思。
第五步：检查答案——在文中找到依据。

十二、常见题型与答题方法

【判断正误 T/F】
回到文中找到对应句子，逐词对比。注意：
- 人名、地名、数字要完全一致
- 时态要对应
- 否定词 not/no/never 不能看漏
- all/both/every 等绝对词要特别注意

【选择题】
先看选项再回文定位。排除明显错误选项，在文中找到依据选最佳答案。

【回答问题】
用完整句子回答，注意人称和时态的转换。
问：What does Tom like? 答：Tom likes playing football.（不用 I）

【填空题】
从文中找原词或稍作变化填入。注意单复数、时态、大小写。

十三、猜词技巧

利用上下文：看这个词前后句子描述的内容推断。
利用构词法：un- 表示"不"（unhappy = 不开心），-er 表示"……的人"（teacher = 教的人），-ful 表示"充满……的"（beautiful = 充满美的）。
利用常识：结合生活经验推断。

第三篇：写作指导与范文
============================================================

十四、写作步骤

审题：写什么主题？什么人称？什么时态？
列要点：写3-5个要点（不遗漏）。
写句子：句子完整，单词拼写正确，注意大小写和标点。
检查：时态是否一致？单复数对不对？标点是否正确？

十五、自我介绍范文

Hello! My name is Li Ming. I am eleven years old. I am a student in Grade Five. I am from Beijing. I am tall and thin. I have big eyes and short hair.
I like playing football and reading books. My favourite subject is English because it is interesting. I don't like math because it is too difficult for me.
My favourite food is ice cream. I don't like vegetables. I know they are healthy, but I think fruit is more delicious.
I have a happy family. There are three people in my family: my father, my mother and me. My father is a doctor. My mother is a teacher. I love them very much.
This is me. Do you want to be my friend?

十六、我的家庭范文

There are four people in my family: my father, my mother, my sister and me.
My father is tall and strong. He is a doctor. He works in a big hospital. He likes playing basketball. He is very kind and always helps others.
My mother is beautiful. She is a teacher. She teaches English in a primary school. She likes cooking. She cooks delicious food for us every day.
My sister is five years old. She is cute and lovely. She likes drawing pictures and watching cartoons.
I am eleven years old. I am a student in Grade Five. I like reading books and playing football.
I love my family. We are very happy together.

十七、我的朋友范文

My best friend is Wang Wei. He is twelve years old. He is tall and strong. He has short black hair and bright eyes.
He is very friendly and helpful. He often helps me with my math. He is good at math and always gets good grades. We often study together after school.
He likes playing basketball very much. He plays basketball every afternoon. He is in the school basketball team. He can run very fast and jump very high.
On weekends, we often go to the park together. Sometimes we ride bikes, sometimes we fly kites. We always have a good time.
I am lucky to have such a good friend. I hope we can be friends forever.

十八、我的周末范文

I had a wonderful weekend.
On Saturday morning, I got up at seven o'clock. After breakfast, I did my homework. In the afternoon, I went to the library with my mother. I borrowed three books. I like reading very much. In the evening, I watched a cartoon on TV. Then I read books for half an hour.
On Sunday morning, my family went to the park. The weather was sunny and warm. We flew kites and had a picnic there. The grass was green and the flowers were beautiful. In the afternoon, I visited my grandparents. My grandma cooked many delicious dishes for us. After dinner, I went home and took a shower.
I went to bed at nine o'clock. I was tired but very happy. What a nice weekend!

十九、看图写话技巧

观察顺序：从上到下，从左到右，从远到近，从人到物。
确定要素：Who（谁）? Where（在哪）? What（做什么）? How（心情怎样）?
时态判断：如果图上有 now/today 用现在时或现在进行时；如果有 yesterday 用过去时。
常用句式：There is/are... In the picture... Look!... They are...
注意：句子要完整，注意大小写和标点（英文句号是 . 不是 。）

二十、书信范文

Dear Amy,
    How are you? I miss you very much.
    Last weekend, I went to the zoo with my family. We saw many animals there. I liked the monkeys best. They were very clever. They could climb up and down. We also saw elephants, tigers and pandas. The pandas were eating bamboo. They were so cute!

    What did you do last weekend? Can you tell me?

                                    Li Ming

第四篇：常考语法对比辨析
============================================================

二十二、易混语法点对比

【be 动词 vs 实义动词】
系表结构用 be：I am happy. She is tall.
实义动词句不用 be：I like apples.（不说 I am like apples.）
He plays football.（不说 He is plays football.）

【some vs any】
some 用于肯定句：I have some apples.
any 用于否定句和疑问句：I don't have any apples. Do you have any books?
例外：征求意见、希望得到肯定回答时，疑问句用 some：Would you like some tea?

【much vs many vs a lot of】
many + 可数名词复数：many books, many students
much + 不可数名词：much water, much time
a lot of / lots of + 都可以：a lot of books, a lot of water

【in/on/at + 时间】
in + 月份/季节/年份/上午下午晚上：in May, in spring, in 2024, in the morning
on + 具体某天/星期/节日：on Monday, on May 1st, on Children's Day
at + 时刻/节日/中午/夜晚：at seven o'clock, at noon, at night, at Christmas
例外：on a cold morning（特指某天的早晨用 on）

【like doing vs like to do】
like doing 表示一般性喜好：I like swimming.（喜欢游泳这件事）
like to do 表示具体某次想做：I'd like to go swimming today.（今天想去游泳）
would like to do = want to do 想要做

【there be vs have/has】
there be 表示"某处存在某物"：There is a book on the desk.
have/has 表示"某人拥有"：I have a book.
不能说 There have a book.（错误！）
就近原则：There is a pen and two books.（靠近 is 的是 a pen 单数）

英语语法就像搭积木的规则。先记住规则，再多造句多练习，规则就会变成习惯。不要怕犯错——犯错误是学习的一部分。每天读一点英语、写几个句子，你的英语就会越来越好。
