# 小学英语核心语法诊断与改错专项

资料性质：原创学习补充材料，不对应某一出版社或教材版本。
适用年级：小学三至六年级。
检索关键词：English grammar, be verbs, do does, have has, tense, third person, plural, question, error correction, 语法改错。

## 1. A complete English sentence

A basic sentence usually needs a subject and a verb.

- I swim every Friday. 我每周五游泳。
- Amy is helpful. 埃米乐于助人。
- The books are on the desk. 书在桌上。

Do not put a form of be directly before an ordinary verb in the simple present.
Wrong: I am play football every day.
Right: I play football every day.

## 2. am, is and are

- I am; he/she/it is; you/we/they are.
- A singular noun usually takes is: The cat is sleepy.
- A plural noun usually takes are: The cats are sleepy.

Negative forms: am not, is not/isn't, are not/aren't.
Questions move the be verb to the front: Is Ben at home? Are the children ready?

## 3. do, does and the third-person singular

In the simple present, I/you/we/they use the base verb. He/she/it usually uses a verb ending in -s or -es.

- I walk to school. Leo walks to school.
- They watch birds. Mia watches birds.
- We study English. Tom studies English.

After does or doesn't, use the base verb.
Wrong: Does she likes music?
Right: Does she like music?

## 4. have and has

- I/you/we/they have.
- He/she/it has.

Examples: We have a new map. Our classroom has two doors.
After does, use have: Does your brother have a bike?

## 5. Nouns and plurals

Common patterns:

- book—books, desk—desks
- bus—buses, box—boxes, watch—watches
- baby—babies, family—families
- leaf—leaves in common school vocabulary
- child—children, man—men, foot—feet, mouse—mice

Some nouns are usually uncountable: water, milk, rice, bread, advice. Say some water or a glass of water, not “a water” when talking about the substance.

## 6. Articles: a, an and the

Use a before a consonant sound and an before a vowel sound.

- a university: university begins with the /j/ consonant sound.
- an hour: hour begins with a vowel sound because h is silent.

Use the when both speaker and listener know which thing is meant: Please close the door.

## 7. Present continuous

Form: am/is/are + verb-ing. It often describes an action happening now.

- I am reading now.
- The dog is running in the garden.
- They are making a model plane.

Spelling examples: make—making, run—running, lie—lying.

Do not forget the be verb.
Wrong: They playing chess.
Right: They are playing chess.

## 8. Simple past

The simple past describes a completed action in the past.

- We visited the museum yesterday.
- She went home at five.
- I did my homework after dinner.

After did or didn't, use the base verb.
Wrong: Did you went there?
Right: Did you go there?

Common irregular verbs: go—went, see—saw, have—had, do—did, eat—ate, take—took, buy—bought, come—came.

## 9. Future expressions

- be going to + base verb: a plan or intention.
- will + base verb: a future statement, decision or prediction.

Examples: We are going to plant trees on Saturday. I think it will rain tonight.

After will, always use the base verb: She will come, not “will comes”.

## 10. Question words

- who asks about a person.
- what asks about a thing or action.
- where asks about a place.
- when asks about time.
- why asks for a reason; answer with because.
- how asks about manner or condition.
- how many asks about countable nouns; how much asks about price or uncountable quantity.

Word order: Question word + helping verb/be verb + subject + other words?
Where do you live? Why is the window open? How many books does he have?

## 11. Diagnostic exercises

Correct each sentence.

1. She are my new classmate.
2. Tom go to the library on Sundays.
3. Does Lily likes apples?
4. My school have a large playground.
5. There are three child in the room.
6. I need a orange for the fruit salad.
7. The boys playing basketball now.
8. We goed to the park yesterday.
9. Did you watched the film last night?
10. He will visits his grandparents tomorrow.
11. How much pencils do you have?
12. Where you are going?

## 12. Answers and reasons

1. She is my new classmate. “She” takes is.
2. Tom goes to the library on Sundays. Third-person singular.
3. Does Lily like apples? Use the base verb after does.
4. My school has a large playground. Singular subject.
5. There are three children in the room. Irregular plural.
6. I need an orange for the fruit salad. Orange begins with a vowel sound.
7. The boys are playing basketball now. Present continuous needs are.
8. We went to the park yesterday. Irregular past form.
9. Did you watch the film last night? Use the base verb after did.
10. He will visit his grandparents tomorrow. Use the base verb after will.
11. How many pencils do you have? Pencils are countable.
12. Where are you going? Move are before the subject.

Editing order: find the time word—find the subject—choose the verb form—check singular/plural—check word order and punctuation.
